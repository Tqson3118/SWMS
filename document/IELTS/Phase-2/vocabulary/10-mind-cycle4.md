# 📝 Từ Vựng — C10: Emotions & Decisions (24 từ, 3 nhóm)

> **Chu kỳ 10** — cảm xúc nào đang cầm lá? Câu ví dụ minh họa cả hai chế độ: **Past Simple** (chuyện khép) và **Present Perfect** (dư âm còn).

## Nhóm 1 — Feelings in the driver's seat (ngày 13)

| Từ | Loại | Nghĩa | Câu ví dụ (B1) |
|----|------|-------|-----------------|
| **mood** /muːd/ | noun | tâm trạng | My mood **decided** that purchase in 2021 — I **have regretted** it ever since. |
| **emotional** /ɪˈməʊʃənl/ | adj | thuộc cảm xúc | That was an emotional decision; this one **has been** calmer. |
| **reason** /ˈriːzn/ | noun | lý lẽ | Reason **has lost** to mood more times than I can count. |
| **gut feeling** | phrase | cảm giác ruột gan | My gut feeling **said no** — and it **has been** right so far. |
| **gut instinct** | phrase | bản năng sơ cấp | I **have trusted** my gut instinct twice this year. |
| **emotional reasoning** | phrase | suy luận bằng cảm xúc | "I feel stupid, so I am stupid" — that **has been** emotional reasoning talking. |
| **calm** /kɑːm/ | adj | bình tĩnh | I **made** the call when I was calm, and I **have thanked** myself since. |
| **panic** /ˈpænɪk/ | verb/noun | hoảng loạn | She **panicked** in March, but **hasn't panicked** since. |

## Nhóm 2 — Regret & fear (ngày 14)

| Từ | Loại | Nghĩa | Câu ví dụ (B1) |
|----|------|-------|-----------------|
| **regret** /rɪˈɡret/ | verb/noun | hối tiếc | I **regretted** it for a year — then I **have made** peace with it. |
| **regretful** /rɪˈɡretfl/ | adj | đầy hối tiếc | That was a regretful "yes" — I **have said** softer ones since. |
| **fear** /fɪə(r)/ | noun | nỗi sợ | Fear **wrote** my last five decisions — no, it **has written** far more. |
| **afraid** /əˈfreɪd/ | adj | sợ | I **was** afraid of the answer, so I **have never asked**. |
| **FOMO** (fear of missing out) | acronym | nỗi sợ bỏ lỡ | FOMO **bought** me that phone; boredom **has owned** it since. |
| **miss out** | phrasal v. | bỏ lỡ | I **have missed out** on sleep, not on that party. |
| **envy** /ˈenvi/ | noun | sự ghen tị | Envy **chose** my university — I **have wondered** ever since. |
| **compare** /kəmˈpeə(r)/ | verb | so sánh | I **compared** myself to everyone at 25; I **have stopped** — mostly. |

## Nhóm 3 — Pressure & overload (ngày 15)

| Từ | Loại | Nghĩa | Câu ví dụ (B1) |
|----|------|-------|-----------------|
| **pressure** /ˈpreʃə(r)/ | noun | áp lực | Pressure **pushed** me into signing — I **have read** contracts slower since. |
| **stress** /stres/ | noun | căng thẳng | Stress **made** that call, not me. |
| **stressed** /strest/ | adj | căng thẳng | I **have been** stressed **since** the deadline moved. |
| **overwhelmed** /ˌəʊvəˈwelmd/ | adj | choáng ngợp | Twenty tabs, zero decisions — I **have been** overwhelmed all week. |
| **choice overload** | phrase | tê liệt vì nhiều lựa chọn | The menu **had** 90 drinks; choice overload **has kept** me ordering the same one. |
| **decision fatigue** | phrase | mệt vì phải quyết | Judges **have been** softer after lunch — decision fatigue is real. |
| **deadline** /ˈdedlaɪn/ | noun | hạn chót | The deadline **forced** the choice; time **has proven** it wrong. |
| **rush** /rʌʃ/ | verb | vội vàng | I **rushed** then; I **have learned** to pause since. |

## Bài tập trộn (24 từ — làm cuối ngày 15)

4. ______ chose my university — I have wondered ever since. (sự ghen tị)
5. The menu had 90 drinks; ______ overload has kept me ordering the same one. (tê liệt vì quá nhiều lựa chọn)
6. I was ______ of the answer, so I have never asked. (sợ)

8. I ______ then; I have learned to pause since. (vội vàng)
