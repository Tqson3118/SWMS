# 📝 Từ Vựng — C21: Stress & Recovery (24 từ, 3 nhóm)

> **Chu kỳ 21** — khi ý chí cạn, cortisol dâng, và thân-tâm cần được phục hồi. Câu ví dụ dùng **Passive reporting** của tuần này (It is said / is believed to...).

## Nhóm 1 — Stress & the body (ngày 9)

| Từ | Loại | Nghĩa | Câu ví dụ (B1) |
|----|------|-------|-----------------|
| **stress** /stres/ | noun | căng thẳng | Stress **is said to** shorten lives. |
| **stressed** /strest/ | adj | căng thẳng (cảm thấy) | Half of workers **are reported to be** stressed daily. |
| **cortisol** /ˈkɔːtɪsɒl/ | noun | cortisol (hormone stress) | Cortisol **is released** when a threat is sensed. |
| **anxiety** /æŋˈzaɪəti/ | noun | lo âu | Anxiety **is believed to** rise with phone use. |
| **anxious** /ˈæŋkʃəs/ | adj | lo âu | Anxious minds **are trained** by constant checking. |
| **burnout** /ˈbɜːnaʊt/ | noun | kiệt sức | Burnout **is recognized** as a medical condition now. |
| **overwhelm** /ˌəʊvəˈwelm/ | verb | choáng ngợp | Students **are overwhelmed** by too many tabs. |
| **exhaustion** /ɪɡˈzɔːstʃən/ | noun | sự kiệt quệ | Exhaustion **is felt** before the body admits it. |

## Bài tập trộn (24 từ — làm cuối ngày 11)
1. Stress is said to ______ lives. (rút ngắn)
2. ______ is recognized as a medical condition now. (kiệt sức)
3. Rest is ______ for laziness. (nhầm)
4. Sleep is ______ the best habit of all. (gọi là)
5. Meditation is ______ to thicken the prefrontal cortex. (được báo cáo)
6. Sunsets are ______ by every human who has healed. (ngắm)
7. Silence is ______ at first; loved later. (sợ)
8. A real break is ______ away from the screen. (dành ra)

---
**ĐÁP ÁN:** 1. shorten 2. Burnout 3. mistaken 4. called 5. reported 6. watched 7. feared 8. taken
