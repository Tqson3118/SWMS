# 🧠 Grammar 18 — Passive Reporting: "It is said that..." (Chu kỳ 21: Day 9-11)

> Cách báo chí và khoa học nói tin đồn / phát hiện mà không nhận trách nhiệm: *"**It is said that** willpower is limited."* / *"The app **is believed to** hook millions."* Thì của truyền thông, của "ai cũng nói", và của nghiên cứu.

---

## Phần A (D9) — It is said that... / It is known that...

### Công thức
| Cấu trúc | Ví dụ |
|----------|-------|
| **It is + V3 + that + clause** | It **is said that** habits take 66 days. |
| Các động từ phổ biến: said, believed, known, reported, thought, claimed, estimated | It **is reported that** 80% of resolutions fail. |
| Quá khứ: It **was said that**... | It **was claimed that** the app was safe. |

### Ý nghĩa
- Người nói KHÔNG nhận trách nhiệm — "ai đó nói".
- Đặc trưng của báo chí: *"It is believed that the experiment was repeated."*

### Bài tập A
A1. Đặt "It is + V3 that":
1. (People believe) dopamine drives behavior. → It ______ ______ ______ dopamine drives behavior.
2. (Scientists estimate) habits take 66 days. → It ______ ______ ______ habits take 66 days.
3. (They reported) the app was addictive. → It ______ ______ ______ the app was addictive.

## Phần B (D10) — S + is said to V

### Công thức
| Cấu trúc | Ví dụ |
|----------|-------|
| **S + is/are + V3 + to + V** | The app **is believed to** hook millions. |
| Quá khứ: was/were + V3 + to have + V3 | The study **was said to have** changed psychology. |
| to be + V3 (passive trong "to" clause) | He **is said to be** a genius. |

### So sánh 2 cấu trúc
| It is said that + clause | S + is said to... |
|--------------------------|-------------------|
| It is said that he is a genius. | He is said to be a genius. |
| It is believed that the app hooks users. | The app is believed to hook users. |

### Bài tập B
B1. Đổi sang "S + is said to...":
1. It is believed that dopamine drives behavior. → Dopamine ______ ______ ______ ______ behavior.
2. It is reported that the app is downloaded daily. → The app ______ ______ ______ ______ daily.
3. It is known that he wrote the book. → He ______ ______ ______ ______ the book.

## Phần C (D11) — Báo chí khoa học

### Mẫu đoạn khoa học (toàn passive)
> "The study **was conducted** at Stanford. Participants **were recruited** through advertisements. The data **was collected** over six weeks. It **is believed** that the results **were influenced** by the setting. The findings **have been published** and **will be discussed** next month."

### Đặc điểm giọng khoa học
- Tránh "I/we" → khách quan.
- Passive + It is said → tránh nhận trách nhiệm cá nhân.

### Bài tập C
C1. Đổi đoạn sau sang giọng khoa học (passive):
> "We recruited 100 people. We collected the data for six weeks. We will publish the results."

C2. Viết 3 câu passive-reporting về 1 nghiên cứu bạn biết (Milgram / Stanford / marshmallow...).

C3. Nói 60 giây: "What is said about habits" — 4 câu It is said/believed/known that...
