# 🧠 Grammar 2 — Past Simple vs Past Continuous (Chu kỳ 2: Day 5-7)

> Cặp thì quan trọng nhất của KỂ CHUYỆN: **Past Continuous = nền (bối cảnh đang chạy)**, **Past Simple = sự kiện (xảy ra ngắn, cắt ngang)**. *"I **was walking** home when I **met** her." — đang đi (nền) thì gặp (sự kiện).

---

## Phần A (D5) — Hành động bị ngắt quãng

### Công thức vàng
| Thành phần | Thì | Ví dụ |
|-----------|-----|-------|
| Hành động DÀI (nền) | Past Continuous | I **was reading** a letter from my childhood |
| + sự kiện NGẮN (cắt ngang) | Past Simple | when the phone **rang**. |

> **Trật tự 2 cách:**
> - *I was reading when the phone rang.* (nền + when + sự kiện)
> - *The phone rang while I was reading.* (sự kiện + while + nền)
> **when + Past Simple** hoặc **while + Past Continuous** — quy tắc cứng.

### Bài tập A
A1. Chia động từ (Past Simple hay Past Continuous):
1. I (walk) ______ home when I (see) ______ an accident.
2. While she (cry) ______, her mother (come) ______ in.
3. When the teacher (arrive) ______, we (talk) ______ loudly.
4. He (break) ______ his leg while he (play) ______ football.
5. While I (live) ______ in Hue, I (meet) ______ my best friend.

A2. Ghép thành câu (2 cách mỗi cặp):
1. (I was sleeping. The alarm rang.) → ______ / ______
2. (She was telling a secret. I overheard.) → ______ / ______

## Phần B (D6) — When vs While

| Liên từ | Đi với | Ví dụ |
|---------|--------|-------|
| **when** | + Past Simple (sự kiện) — NHƯNG cũng đứng đầu mệnh nền | The bell rang **when** I was bathing. · **When** I was young, I was shy. |
| **while** | + Past Continuous (nền) | **While** I was bathing, the bell rang. |

> **Mẹo an toàn:** để chắc đúng — *while + was/were V-ing* và *when + V-ed*. Đây là công thức không bao giờ sai ở B1.

### Bài tập B
B1. Điền when hoặc while:
1. ______ I was cooking, my child was drawing. (song song)
2. The lights went out ______ we were having dinner. (sự kiện)
3. ______ she opened the door, everyone shouted "Surprise!"
4. He fell asleep ______ he was studying. 

B2. Sửa lỗi:
1. While I watched TV, she called. → ______
2. When I was opened the letter, I cried. → ______

## Phần C (D7) — Hành động song song + trộn

### Hai nền chạy cùng lúc (cả 2 Past Continuous)
- While I **was cooking**, my husband **was cleaning**. (cả hai đều đang diễn ra)
- We **were laughing** while they **were arguing**. (tương phản)

### Bảng quyết định cuối cùng
| Tình huống | Cấu trúc | Ví dụ |
|-----------|----------|-------|
| Nền + sự kiện ngắt | was V-ing + when + V-ed | I was driving when it started to rain. |
| Sự kiện + nền | V-ed + while + was V-ing | It started to rain while I was driving. |
| 2 nền song song | was V-ing + while + was V-ing | She was crying while he was apologizing. |
| Chuỗi sự kiện | toàn Past Simple | I woke up, made tea, and opened the letter. |

### Bài tập C
C1. Chọn đúng:
1. (While / When) I was a child, we lived by the sea.
2. She (was smiling / smiled) when I (was looking / looked) at her.
3. While he (repaired / was repairing) the bike, it (started / was starting) to rain.

C2. Kể 1 sự cố ngắn (5 câu): mở đầu bằng nền (was V-ing) → sự kiện cắt ngang (V-ed) → chuỗi phản ứng (V-ed) → cảm xúc. Ví dụ: "Last Sunday, I was riding my bike..."

## ✅ ĐÁP ÁN

**A1:** 1. was walking / saw 2. was crying / came 3. arrived / were talking 4. broke / was playing 5. was living / met
**A2:** 1. I was sleeping when the alarm rang. / The alarm rang while I was sleeping. 2. She was telling a secret when I overheard. / I overheard while she was telling a secret.
**B1:** 1. While 2. while (when cũng chấp nhận: when + nền) 3. When 4. while
**B2:** 1. While I was watching TV, she called. 2. When I opened the letter, I cried. (bỏ was — sự kiện)
**C1:** 1. When 2. was smiling / looked (hoặc smiled / was looking — nghĩa hơi khác, cả hai hợp lệ) 3. was repairing / started
