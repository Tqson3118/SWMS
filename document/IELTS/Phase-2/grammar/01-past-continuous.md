# 🧠 Grammar 1 — Past Continuous (Chu kỳ 1: Day 1-3)

> **Past Continuous** = quá khứ tiếp diễn: việc ĐANG diễn ra tại một thời điểm trong quá khứ. *"At 8 p.m. last night, I **was reading** a book about memory."* — nền của câu chuyện quá khứ.

---

## Phần A (D1) — Form + Meaning

### Công thức
| Chủ ngữ | Cấu trúc | Ví dụ (chủ đề ký ức) |
|---------|-----------|--------------------------|
| I / he / she / it | **was** + V-ing | I **was remembering** my childhood. · She **was crying** quietly. |
| you / we / they | **were** + V-ing | We **were talking** about the past. · They **were living** in Hue then. |

### Khi nào dùng?
| Dùng | Ví dụ | Tín hiệu |
|------|-------|----------|
| Hành động ĐANG diễn ra tại 1 thời điểm quá khứ | At 9 p.m., I **was studying**. | at + giờ + (last night) |
| Bối cảnh/nền của câu chuyện | The sun **was setting**. Birds **were singing**. | mở đầu chuyện |
| Hành động song song (while) | While she **was cooking**, he **was cleaning**. | while |

> **So với Past Simple:** Past Simple = sự kiện NGẮN, đã xong (I read a book). Past Continuous = tiến trình ĐANG CHẠY (I was reading — chưa xong lúc đó).

### Bài tập A
A1. Điền was/were + V-ing:
1. At 10 p.m., I (think) ______ ______ ______ about my childhood.
2. We (live) ______ ______ ______ in a small village then.
3. She (not/cry) ______ ______ ______ — she was cutting onions.
4. (you/sleep) ______ ______ ______ at midnight?
5. The children (play) ______ ______ ______ outside all afternoon.

A2. Chọn Past Simple hay Past Continuous:
1. Yesterday at 7 a.m., I (cooked / was cooking) breakfast.
2. She (read / was reading) the letter and (smiled / was smiling).
3. Last night I (watched / was watching) a movie about memory.

## Phần B (D2) — Phủ định + Câu hỏi

| Dạng | Ví dụ |
|------|-------|
| Phủ định | I **wasn't thinking** about that. · They **weren't listening** to me. |
| Câu hỏi Yes/No | **Were** you **sleeping**? — Yes, I **was**. / No, I **wasn't**. |
| Câu hỏi Wh- | What **were** you **doing** at 9 p.m.? · Where **was** she **going**? |

> Lỗi kinh điển: dùng was/were riêng mà thiếu V-ing (❌ I was remember → ✅ I was remembering).

### Bài tập B
B1. Chuyển sang phủ định rồi câu hỏi:
1. She was smiling. → phủ định: ______ / câu hỏi: ______
2. They were arguing about the past. → phủ định: ______ / câu hỏi (What...?): ______

B2. Sửa lỗi:
1. I was remember my childhood. → ______
2. She was not cried. → ______
3. Were you study last night? → ______

## Phần C (D3) — When/While + mốc thời gian quá khứ

### While + Past Continuous (hành động nền)
- **While** I **was walking** home, I saw an old friend. (đi bộ = nền dài; saw = sự kiện ngắn)
- **While** we **were talking**, the phone rang.

### At + thời điểm (khung cảnh)
- **At midnight**, everyone **was sleeping**.
- **This time last year**, I **was living** in Da Nang.

### Bài tập C
C1. Nối cặp câu bằng while:
1. (I was doing my homework. My brother was playing games.) → ______
2. (She was telling a story. The children were listening.) → ______

C2. Viết 3 câu về BẠN tối qua (9 p.m. / nửa đêm / lúc mặt trời lặn):
- At 9 p.m. last night, I was... (3 câu, Past Continuous)

C3. Dịch:
1. Lúc 8 giờ tối qua, tôi đang đọc sách. → ______
2. Khi tôi đang nấu ăn, cô ấy gọi. → ______
3. Hôm ấy trời đang mưa. → ______

## ✅ ĐÁP ÁN

**A1:** 1. was thinking 2. were living 3. wasn't crying 4. Were you sleeping 5. were playing
**A2:** 1. was cooking 2. was reading / smiled (hoặc: read / was smiling — tùy ý nhấn) 3. was watching
**B1:** 1. She wasn't smiling. / Was she smiling? 2. They weren't arguing... / What were they arguing about?
**B2:** 1. I was remembering my childhood. 2. She was not crying. 3. Were you studying last night?
**C1:** 1. While I was doing my homework, my brother was playing games. 2. While she was telling a story, the children were listening.
**C3:** 1. At 8 p.m. last night, I was reading a book. 2. While I was cooking, she called. 3. It was raining that day.
