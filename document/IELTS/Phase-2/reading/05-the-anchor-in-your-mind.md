# 📖 Đọc 2 — The Anchor in Your Mind (~330 từ)

> Chủ đề: **anchoring** — con số đầu tiên neo mọi phán đoán sau nó. Đánh dấu các cụm for/since · already/yet.

---

Before you read the rest, answer quickly: is the tallest tree in the world taller or shorter than 1,200 meters? And how tall do you think it is?

You said something between 400 and 900 meters, probably. The real answer is about 116 meters. Did the 1,200 matter? You knew it was wrong — and it still **has moved** your guess, silently, upward. That number was an anchor.

Kahneman and Amos Tversky **have shown** this trick again and again since the 1970s. They spun a wheel of fortune in front of students — a rigged wheel that stopped on 10 or 65 — then asked: what percentage of African countries are in the UN? The students who saw 65 guessed higher than the ones who saw 10. A random number **has steered** real judgments, and nobody felt it happen.

Since then, the anchor **has spread** into every market. Shops **have been using** it forever: cross out 2,000,000, write 899,000, and the brain screams *bargain*. The seller never says the old price was real — it doesn't matter. The first number **has already done** its work before the negotiation even begins.

You **have met** this anchor in salary talks, apartment prices, and "only 3 left in stock". You will meet it again this week.

Can you defend yourself? Only partly. Anchoring influences even people who know the effect by name — decades of empirical research **have demonstrated** that. What you can do is simpler: when a number arrives, pause, and build your own estimate from your own facts *before* the other number settles in your head.

The mind needs a first number to think against. The question is only ever: whose?

**Câu hỏi:**
1. What happened to the readers' guesses about the tree because of "1,200 meters"?
2. What did the wheel experiment show?
3. Why can't knowledge of anchoring fully protect you?

2. Even a random number (10 or 65 from a rigged wheel) steered serious estimates about African countries in the UN.
