# 📖 Đọc 1 — The Two Systems in Your Head (~350 từ)

> Bài mở màn tháng 2 — gạch chân mỗi câu Present Perfect (have/has + V3) bạn tìm thấy.

---

Psychologists have argued about the mind for centuries, but one idea has survived every battle: there are two ways of thinking, and they live in the same head.

Daniel Kahneman, who **has spent** his whole career studying how people choose, gave them plain names: System 1 and System 2. System 1 is fast, automatic, and emotional. It **has kept** our ancestors alive for millions of years — it hears the rustle, sees the snake, and moves the body before "thinking" even starts. System 2 is slow, lazy, and logical. It does the taxes, compares the phone plans, and writes the apology email at midnight.

Here is the problem: System 2 is expensive. It burns energy and attention, so the brain calls it only when it must. Most of the day, System 1 drives. It **has been driving** since you woke up — you chose your breakfast, your route, and your first opinion of this article without any real debate.

Sometimes that works beautifully. A chess master **has trained** his System 1 so well that the right move simply appears. A nurse **has seen** a thousand faces and knows instantly when one is wrong.

But sometimes System 1 answers a question nobody asked. Ask yourself: is this person smart? System 1 hears "is this person confident?" and answers that instead — loudly, and with total certainty. Then it **has finished** its job, and it goes back to sleep, proud of itself.

The skill of the modern mind is not "being rational". Nobody **has ever managed** that. The skill is noticing the moment when a fast answer rises in you, and asking one small question: *is this the right question?*

That pause — one breath, one second — is where System 2 **has always lived**. You have just met it. Keep the address.

**Câu hỏi:**
1. What are the two systems, and what does each one do?
2. Why does the brain prefer System 1?
3. According to the article, what is the real skill of the modern mind?

<details><summary>ĐÁP ÁN (nhấn để mở)</summary>

1. System 1 = fast, automatic, emotional (survival reactions, instant opinions); System 2 = slow, effortful, logical (taxes, comparisons, careful writing).
2. Because System 2 is expensive — it burns energy and attention, so it is called only when necessary.
3. Not "being rational" (nobody has managed that), but noticing when a fast answer rises and asking whether it answers the right question.
