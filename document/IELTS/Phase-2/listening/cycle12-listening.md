## 3. Supplemental authored writing and speaking practice (5-10 phút; không có audio nguồn)

**Prompt:** Explain one decision you have made and one heuristic you have noticed. Use one present-perfect sentence and one sentence with a specific past time.

**Model response (authored, not transcript):** “I have changed one spending habit this year. Last month, I noticed that a familiar brand influenced my choice before I compared the alternatives. I now pause and check the numbers.”

**Rubric (4 points):** 1 point for a clear decision; 1 for an accurate present-perfect form; 1 for a specific past-time sentence; 1 for explaining a heuristic without claiming that every decision is irrational. The model is a language example only, not a source quotation.
