### Questions (source-dependent)

1. What cultural imbalance does Guy Winch identify between physical health and emotional health?
2. Why does Winch consider chronic loneliness as physically hazardous as cigarette smoking?
3. How does persistent rumination after setbacks harm emotional well-being?
4. What is the primary purpose of practicing emotional hygiene in daily life?

<details>
<summary>Hidden answer key (open after attempting)</summary>

1. Society treats physical cuts and scrapes immediately, but routinely ignores psychological pain and trauma.
2. Loneliness suppresses immune system function, increases stress hormones, and raises mortality risk.
3. Replaying negative experiences reinforces cognitive despair, deepens anxiety, and prolongs distress.
4. Treating psychological wounds promptly to protect self-esteem and build durable emotional resilience.

</details>
