### Questions (source-dependent)

1. What does Barry Schwartz describe as the 'official dogma' of Western industrial societies?
2. What paradox occurs when consumers are presented with an overwhelming number of options?
3. Why does having many alternatives increase regret and escalation of expectations?
4. What is the difference between a maximizer and a satisficer according to the talk?

<details>
<summary>Hidden answer key (open after attempting)</summary>

1. The belief that maximizing individual freedom requires maximizing the number of choices available.
2. Rather than liberating people, excessive options produce analysis paralysis and reduce satisfaction with the choice made.
3. People easily imagine attractive features of forgone options, leading to higher expectations and self-blame.
4. Maximizers aim for the absolute best outcome across all choices, whereas satisficers settle happily once criteria are met.

</details>
