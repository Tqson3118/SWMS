### Questions (source-dependent)

1. Why do personal disagreements frequently become unproductive according to Julia Dhar?
2. How does establishing a shared reality help opposing parties engage in productive debate?
3. Why is separating ideas from personal identity essential for open-minded discussion?
4. What is the humility of uncertainty and how does it improve decision-making?

<details>
<summary>Hidden answer key (open after attempting)</summary>

1. People become defensively attached to winning arguments rather than pursuing constructive mutual progress.
2. Finding a baseline of common principles creates collaborative ground before tackling points of dispute.
3. Detaching identity from opinions prevents defensiveness and allows objective evaluation of evidence.
4. Acknowledging that one might be mistaken encourages curious inquiry and better synthesis of evidence.

</details>

# 🎧 Listening Day 3 (Phase 2 — Month 3) — Persuasion

> B1 listening: nghe về Cialdini / quảng cáo — six principles of persuasion.

- **Primary:** TED: Julia Dhar — "How to disagree productively and find common ground" — https://www.ted.com/talks/julia_dhar_how_to_disagree_productively_and_find_common_ground

- **Transcript status:** Available
- **Date checked:** 2026-09-22
- **Level:** B1
- **Listening goal:** Nhận diện 6 nguyên tắc thuyết phục và phương pháp tranh luận tìm tiếng nói chung
- **Fallback keyword:** TED Julia Dhar disagree productively persuasion

## 2. Shadowing (15-20 phút) — 5 câu
1. If someone gives you a gift, you feel you owe them.
2. If you said yes once, you will say yes again.
3. If everyone bought it, you would want it too.
4. If it were the last one, you would grab it.
5. If a doctor said it, you would believe it.

Cách làm: nghe 1 câu → tạm dừng → nói lại → lặp 3 lần/câu.
