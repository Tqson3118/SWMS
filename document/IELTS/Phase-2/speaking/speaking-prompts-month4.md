# 🗣️ Speaking Prompts — Phase 2 Month 4 (Habits & Behavior Change)

> Mỗi ngày 3-5 phút với AI voice (prompt 1 trong `Resources/ai-conversation-guide.md`). Xong LUÔN chạy prompt 2 (sửa lỗi) → chép 3 lỗi vào `Phase-1/error-log.md`. Tháng này: MÔ TẢ QUY TRÌNH + KỂ HÀNH TRÌNH THÓI QUEN — passive cho khách quan, mọi thì phase 2 cho câu chuyện.

| Ngày | Chủ đề | Gợi ý câu |
|------|--------|-----------|
| D1 | My worst loop | My habit was built by... The reward is delivered when... |
| D2 | How my phone hooks me | The app is designed to... I am hooked by... |
| D3 | A habit I was taught | I was told to... by my mother. It has been kept since. |
| D4 | (test) | — |
| D5 | The four steps | First, the cue is noticed. Then... Finally... |
| D6 | Who designed my day? | My morning is designed by... — not by me. |
| D7 | One thing that was removed | The app was uninstalled... and nothing was lost. |
| D8 | (test) | — |
| D9 | It is said that... | It is said that habits take 66 days. In my case... |
| D10 | Stress story | By the time I noticed, I had been burned out. |
| D11 | My recovery plan | Sleep is called... Walks are prescribed by... |
| D12 | (TEST TỔNG #1) | Kể 1 quy trình 2 phút |
| D13 | Willpower myth | Willpower is treated like a battery — but my room is arranged... |
| D14 | My marshmallow | I was offered... I ate it. Twice. Now I know why. |
| D15 | Friction audit | Friction is added to... and removed from... |
| D16 | (test) | — |
| D17 | A habit I have redesigned | My phone-checking was built by years of... One Sunday, I deleted... Since then... |
| D18 | My vote | I am becoming a person who... Each morning, one vote is cast. |

| D20 | (test) | — |

> **Mẹo tháng này:** mỗi câu passive xong đuôi câu active cá nhân — *"The app was designed to hook me — but I deleted it."* Khách quan + cá nhân = giọng B1 thật.
