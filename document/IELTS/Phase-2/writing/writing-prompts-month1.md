## Checklist tự sửa (tháng 1 Phase 2)

1. Nền = Past Continuous (was/were + V-ing)? Sự kiện = Past Simple?
2. while + nền, when + sự kiện?
3. used to + V nguyên mẫu; didn't USE to (không -d)?
4. Trạng thái (be/have/live) KHÔNG dùng would?
5. Đoạn có mở-kết rõ (One evening... / Looking back...)?

## Prompt chấm bài cuối tuần

```text
You are my English teacher. Check this B1 narrative paragraph about memory/past. Focus on:
1) Past Continuous vs Past Simple (background vs events), 2) used to / would, 3) connectors and story flow.
Correct ALL mistakes and explain each briefly in Vietnamese. Then rewrite my text in better English
(natural B1 level). Give me a score 0-10.
My text: [dán bài viết]
```
