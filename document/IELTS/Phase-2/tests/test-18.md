**Q5:** 1. conformity 2. obedience 3. persuasion 4. bystander 5. groupthink 6. stereotype 7. boundary 8. skeptical 9. reciprocity 10. scarcity
**Q6:** (mẫu) 1. In a group, each person's share of responsibility shrinks — so nobody helps. 2. A group that wants unity more than accuracy — doubts get swallowed. 3. A small yes first makes a bigger yes easier later. 4. Shared problems that neither group can solve alone — they turn enemies into teammates. 5. When a label is made salient, the labeled people perform worse in that domain.
**Q7:** 1. Human beings are built to synchronize — the question is with what. 2. They are interrupts — a pause, a named person, a question, a sentence of dissent. 3. For expensive moments — "if I were in that room..." is practice for the real room.
