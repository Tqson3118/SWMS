**ĐÁP ÁN:**
**Q1:** 1. had signed / entered (nhấn "đã ký rồi mới vào"; chấp nhận signed / entered vì after đã chỉ rõ thứ tự) 2. explained / hadn't told (nhấn "đã giấu thông tin") 3. arrived / had already begun 4. opened / stepped / froze (chuỗi liền — 3 Past Simple) 5. stopped (sự kiện chính: "He stopped it on day six")
**Q2:** 1. When I arrived, the experiment had already started. 2. She went to the seminar yesterday. 3. After he ate, he left. 4. By the time we came, the crowd had dispersed. 5. I had known the answer.
**Q3:** 1. had converted/turned 2. assigned 3. became 4. broke 5. stopped
**Q4:** 1. designed / invited (thứ tự rõ — 2 Past Simple; hoặc had designed / invited) 2. had learned / knew 3. had started / died
**Q5:** 1. Before I opened my mouth, the group had already agreed. 2. She had never seen anything like it before. 3. After he had signed, he read it.
**Q6:** 1. bystander effect 2. groupthink 3. herd mentality 4. stereotype 5. prejudice
**Q7:** 1. bystander 2. groupthink 3. deindividuation 4. stereotype 5. dissent
**Q8:** 1. superordinate goals (mục tiêu chung) 2. moved/changed 3. When a label is made salient, the labeled people perform worse in that domain.
