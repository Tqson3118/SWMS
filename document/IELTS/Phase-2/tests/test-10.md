5. (Did you ever eat / Have you ever eaten) fried insects?

**Q2. for + thì (10đ):**
1. I (live) ______ in Hanoi for two years — then I moved. (khép)
2. I (live) ______ in Hanoi for two years — still here. (mở)
3. She (work) ______ there from 2015 to 2019. (khép)
4. He (be) ______ my mentor for a decade — and still is. (mở)
5. They (date) ______ for six months before they broke up. (khép)
