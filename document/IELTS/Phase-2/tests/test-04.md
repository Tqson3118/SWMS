## Grammar (50đ)
**Q1. Liên từ thời gian (15đ, 3đ):** when/while/as soon as/before/after
1. ______ I finished my homework, I went out to play. 2. ______ she was sleeping, someone knocked softly. 3. ______ he heard the news, he called his mother. 4. We hugged ______ saying goodbye. 5. I locked the door ______ leaving.

**Q2. Trạng từ nhịp (15đ, 3đ):** suddenly/eventually/finally/at first/meanwhile
1. ______, I thought the letter was lost. But it was under the bed! 2. We waited an hour. ______, we decided to walk. 3. I was reading quietly. ______, the lights went out. 4. The mother was cooking. ______, the children were drawing. 5. After 10 years, she ______ returned to her hometown.

**Q3. Ghép (10đ, 2đ):**
1. (I read the letter. I understood everything.) as soon as → ______
2. (She cried. Her father held her.) while → ______

**Q4. Sửa lỗi (10đ, 2đ):**
1. After I was finishing work, I went home. → ______
2. As soon as I was seeing her, I remembered. → ______
3. She was opening the door as soon as she heard the knock. (đúng/sai?) → ______
4. Before to leave, I locked the door. → ______
5. While I was a child, I would be shy. (trạng thái) → ______
