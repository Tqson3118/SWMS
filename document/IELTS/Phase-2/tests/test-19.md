## Grammar (50đ)
**Q1. Điền passive đúng thì (10đ):**
1. The habit loop (describe) ______ ______ ______ by researchers in the 1990s. (Past Simple)
2. New habits (form) ______ ______ slowly. (Present Simple)
3. My screen time (reduce) ______ ______ ______ ______ by half. (Present Perfect)
4. The results (publish) ______ ______ ______ next month. (Future)
5. The app (update) ______ ______ ______ ______ right now. (Present Cont.)

**Q2. Đổi active → passive (10đ):**
1. Dopamine drives the loop. → ______
2. They have studied habits for decades. → ______
3. She wrote the report yesterday. → ______
4. The company will launch the app. → ______
5. Zimbardo conducted the study. → ______

**Q3. Sửa lỗi (10đ, 2đ):**
1. The app is design to hook you. → ______
2. Habits are built by repeat. → ______
3. The study was conduct in 1972. → ______
4. My phone was stole. → ______
5. The accident was happened at midnight. → ______

**Q4. Chọn thì đúng cho be (10đ):**
1. The cue (is / was) noticed before you act. (quy trình chung)
2. My routine (is / has been) changed since March. (đến giờ)
3. The study (was / is) conducted in 1961. (mốc khép)
4. The reward (will be / will been) delivered instantly.
5. The data (is being / is been) collected right now.

**Q5. Dịch (10đ):**
