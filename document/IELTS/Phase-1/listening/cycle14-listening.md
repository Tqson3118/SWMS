## Questions (answer reveal separate)

1. What does Neil say a **family tree** shows?
2. What did Beth’s mother find when she made their family tree?
3. Why is Neil’s surname useful for tracing his ancestors?
4. What does **ancestry** mean in Beth’s explanation?
