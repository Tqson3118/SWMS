# 🧠 Grammar 10 — Comparatives: So Sánh Hơn (Chu kỳ 13: Day 1-3)

> "She is **taller** than me" · "I am **more patient** than my brother" · "Love is **stronger** than fear". So sánh hơn — công cụ đo lường con người và mọi thứ.

---

## Phần A (D1) — -er vs more

| Loại tính từ | Quy tắc | Ví dụ |
|--------------|----------|-------|
| Ngắn (1 âm tiết) | + **-er** + than | tall → tall**er** · fast → fast**er** · old → old**er** |
| Kết thúc -y (2 âm tiết) | đổi y → **-ier** | easy → eas**ier** · happy → happ**ier** · early → earl**ier** |
| Dài (2+ âm tiết) | **more** + adj + than | patient → **more** patient · careful → **more** careful · intelligent → **more** intelligent |

> **Cấu trúc:** A + be + comparative + **than** + B. *He is taller than his brother.* · *She is more confident than me.*

### Bài tập A
A1. Viết dạng so sánh:
tall → ___ · happy → ___ · patient → ___ · strong → ___ · easy → ___ · careful → ___ · fast → ___ · friendly → ___ · confident → ___ · big → ___

A2. Điền đúng dạng:
1. My sister is (outgoing) ______ than me.
2. This exercise is (easy) ______ than the last one.
3. He is (generous) ______ than his brother.
4. I am (calm) ______ than my mother.
5. Cats are (independent) ______ than dogs.

---

## Phần B (D2) — Bất quy tắc + so sánh với danh từ

| Tính từ | So sánh hơn | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|---------|-------------|-------|-------|
| good | **better** | tốt hơn | Her English is better than mine. |
| bad | **worse** | tệ hơn | His temper is worse than mine. |
| far | **farther / further** | xa hơn | Psychology goes further than behavior. |
| little | **less** | ít hơn | less patience, less time |
| much/many | **more** | nhiều hơn | more love, more words |

> **more/less/fewer + danh từ:** *more trust* (U) · *more kisses* (C) · *less patience* (U) · *fewer arguments* (C — fewer luôn đi với danh từ đếm được, less với không đếm!).

### Bài tập B
B1. Điền better/worse/more/less/fewer:
1. My old phone was ______ than my new one. (tệ)
2. This book is ______ than that one. (tốt)
3. We need ______ promises and ______ action. (nhiều lời hứa hơn — KHÔNG; nhiều hành động hơn)
4. I have ______ free time than last year. (ít hơn — time là U)
5. There were ______ people at the party than we expected. (ít hơn — people là C)

B2. Sửa lỗi:
1. She is more taller than me. → ______
2. He is patienter than his father. → ______
3. This test is more easy. → ______

## Phần C (D3) — as...as, less...than, much/a bit + comparative

### Công thức bổ sung
| Công thức | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|-----------|-------|-------|
| **as + adj + as** | bằng... | She is **as calm as** her grandmother. |
| **not as/so + adj + as** | không bằng | I am **not as patient as** you. |
| **less + adj + than** | kém hơn | He is **less talkative than** his sister. |
| **much / a bit + comparative** | hơn nhiều / hơn một chút | much better · a bit louder · far more important |

### Bài tập C
C1. Viết lại dùng as...as:
1. My brother is 1.80m. I am 1.80m. (tall) → We are ______
2. She is very kind. Her mother is very kind. → She is ______

C2. Điền much/a bit:
1. This coffee is ______ better than that one. (hơn nhiều)
2. He is ______ taller than me. (cao hơn một chút)

C3. Dịch:
1. Cô ấy kiên nhẫn hơn tôi. → ______
2. Anh ấy không chăm chỉ bằng em trai. → ______
3. Tình yêu quan trọng hơn tiền. → ______
4. Tôi hạnh phúc hơn năm ngoái nhiều. → ______

## ✅ ĐÁP ÁN

**A1:** taller · happier · more patient · stronger · easier · more careful · faster · friendlier · more confident · bigger
**A2:** 1. more outgoing 2. easier 3. more generous 4. calmer 5. more independent
**B1:** 1. worse 2. better 3. fewer / more 4. less 5. fewer
**B2:** 1. She is taller than me. (bỏ more) 2. He is more patient than his father. 3. This test is easier.
**C1:** 1. as tall as each other / the same height 2. as kind as her mother
**C2:** 1. much 2. a bit
**C3:** 1. She is more patient than me. 2. He is not as hard-working as his brother. 3. Love is more important than money. 4. I am much happier than last year.
