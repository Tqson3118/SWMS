# 🧠 Grammar 19 — Conjunctions: Liên Từ (Chu kỳ 23: Day 17-19)

> Liên từ NEO các ý lại: *and* (và), *but* (nhưng), *or* (hoặc), *so* (nên), *because* (vì), *although* (mặc dù), *when* (khi), *if* (nếu). Học xong chu kỳ này bạn viết được câu PHỨC — nhảy cấp từ câu đơn.

---

## Phần A (D17) — and / but / or

| Liên từ | Chức năng | Ví dụ |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| **and** | cộng ý (cùng hướng) | She is kind **and** honest. · I smiled **and** waved. |
| **but** | tương phản (ngược hướng) | He is rich **but** lonely. · I called her, **but** she didn't answer. |
| **or** | lựa chọn | Tea **or** coffee? · We can talk now **or** later. |

> Vị trí: giữa 2 ý ngang cấp. Nếu chủ ngữ lặp lại có thể lược: *She smiled and (she) waved.*

### Bài tập A
A1. Điền and/but/or:
1. I want to come, ______ I'm busy. 2. Would you like rice ______ noodles? 3. He is shy ______ very kind. 4. We can walk ______ take a taxi. 5. She opened the letter ______ started to read. 6. I love her, ______ she loves someone else.

A2. Nối 2 câu thành 1 bằng liên từ:
1. He is 60. He still runs every morning. (but) → ______
2. You can apologize. You can lose this friendship. (or) → ______

## Phần B (D18) — so / because: nguyên nhân & kết quả

| Liên từ | Chức năng | Ví dụ |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| **because** | đưa LÝ DO | She cried **because** the movie was sad. |
| **so** | đưa KẾT QUẢ | The movie was sad, **so** she cried. |

> ⚠️ **Bẫy tiếng Việt:** "Vì... nên..." dùng CẢ HAI trong tiếng Việt — nhưng tiếng Anh chỉ dùng MỘT: *Because it rained, we stayed home.* HOẶC *It rained, so we stayed home.* (❌ Because it rained, so we...).

### Bài tập B
B1. Điền because/so:
1. I was tired, ______ I went to bed early. 2. I went to bed early ______ I was tired. 3. He lied to her, ______ she left him. 4. She left him ______ he lied. 5. It was raining, ______ we canceled the picnic.

B2. Sửa lỗi:
1. Because I was sick, so I stayed home. → ______
2. Because she smiled, so I felt better. → ______

B3. Ghép cặp: (1) He didn't sleep. (2) He's exhausted. → dùng because + dùng so (2 câu).

## Phần C (D19) — although / when / if: câu phức

| Liên từ | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|---------|-------|-------|
| **although** | mặc dù (tương phản) | **Although** he is shy, he speaks well. · She forgave him, **although** he hurt her. |
| **when** | khi (thời gian) | I was sad **when** she left. · **When** you need help, call me. |
| **if** | nếu (điều kiện) | **If** you love someone, tell them. · I'll help you **if** I can. |

> Ngữ pháp mệnh đề: mỗi mệnh đề có chủ ngữ + V riêng — liên từ đứng đầu mệnh đề phụ. *When she arrived, we started dinner.* (2 mệnh đề, 2 động từ).

### Bài tập C
C1. Điền although/when/if:
1. ______ you smile, your brain feels happier. 2. He kept working ______ he was exhausted. 3. I was a child ______ I first saw the sea. 4. ______ you don't try, you will never know. 5. She stayed calm ______ she was angry inside.

C2. Ghép thành câu phức:
1. (You practice daily. Your English will improve.) if → ______
2. (It was raining. They kept walking.) although → ______
3. (I heard her voice. I felt calm.) when → ______

C3. Dịch:
1. Mặc dù anh ấy già, nhưng ông ấy mạnh mẽ. → ______ (chỉ dùng although)
2. Nếu bạn cần tôi, hãy gọi. → ______
3. Khi tôi buồn, tôi nghe nhạc. → ______

## ✅ ĐÁP ÁN

**A1:** 1. but 2. or 3. but 4. or 5. and 6. but
**A2:** 1. He is 60, but he still runs every morning. 2. You can apologize, or you can lose this friendship.
**B1:** 1. so 2. because 3. so 4. because 5. so
**B2:** 1. Because I was sick, I stayed home. (hoặc: I was sick, so I stayed home.) 2. Because she smiled, I felt better. (hoặc: She smiled, so I felt better.)
**B3:** He's exhausted because he didn't sleep. / He didn't sleep, so he's exhausted.
**C1:** 1. When 2. although 3. when 4. If 5. although
**C2:** 1. If you practice daily, your English will improve. 2. Although it was raining, they kept walking. 3. When I heard her voice, I felt calm.
**C3:** 1. Although he is old, he is strong. 2. If you need me, call. 3. When I am sad, I listen to music.
