# 🧠 Grammar 11 — Superlatives: So Sánh Nhất (Chu kỳ 14: Day 5-7)

> "She is **the kindest** person I know" · "This is **the most important** lesson of my life". Superlative = đỉnh nhất, tệ nhất — dùng khi nói về người/bài học/quyết định quan trọng nhất.

---

## Phần A (D5) — the -est vs the most

| Loại tính từ | Quy tắc | Ví dụ |
|--------------|----------|-------|
| Ngắn (1 âm tiết) | **the** + adj + **-est** | tall → the tall**est** · smart → the smart**est** |
| Kết thúc -y | y → **-iest** | happy → the happ**iest** · easy → the eas**iest** |
| Dài (2+ âm tiết) | **the most** + adj | beautiful → the most beautiful · patient → the most patient |

> ⚠️ **Luôn có THE** trước superlative: *She is **the** kindest person.* (thiếu the = lỗi kinh điển!)

### Bài tập A
A1. Viết dạng superlative:
tall → ___ · happy → ___ · patient → ___ · good → ___ · bad → ___ · interesting → ___ · kind → ___ · confident → ___ · easy → ___ · important → ___

A2. Điền:
1. My grandmother is the (wise) ______ person in our family.
2. This is the (important) ______ lesson of my life.
3. He is the (funny) ______ guy in our class.
4. That was the (bad) ______ day of the year.
5. She is the (hard-working) ______ student I know.

## Phần B (D6) — Bất quy tắc + the least

| Tính từ | Superlative | Ví dụ |
|---------|-------------|-------|
| good | **the best** | You are the best friend ever. |
| bad | **the worst** | That was the worst fight we ever had. |
| far | **the farthest/furthest** | the furthest corner of my heart |

> **The least** = ít... nhất (ngược của the most): *the least interesting chapter* · *the least patient person*.

### Bài tập B
B1. Điền the best/the worst/the least:
1. This café has ______ coffee in town. (tốt nhất)
2. Monday is ______ day of the week for me. (tệ nhất)
3. He is ______ talkative person in the family. (ít nói nhất)

B2. Trả lời về BẠN (câu đầy đủ):
1. Who is the kindest person you know?
2. What was the best day of your life so far?
3. What is the most difficult thing about learning English?

## Phần C (D7) — Trộn comparative & superlative

| | Comparative (2 đối tượng) | Superlative (3+ đối tượng) |
|-|--------------------------|------------------------------|
| Ví dụ | Mai is **taller than** Lan. | Mai is **the tallest** in the class. |
| | This book is **more interesting than** that one. | This is **the most interesting** book of the year. |

> **Mẹo nhận biết:** thấy "than" → comparative; thấy "in/of/my life" → superlative. *the best day **of my life*** · *the smartest person **in the room***.

### Bài tập C
C1. Chọn comparative hay superlative:
1. My father is (taller / the tallest) than my mother.
2. She is (more patient / the most patient) person in the family.
3. This movie was (better / the best) than the first one.
4. That was (worse / the worst) mistake of my life.
5. I am (more confident / the most confident) than last year.

C2. Sửa lỗi:
1. She is most kind person I know. → ______
2. He is the most tallest in the class. → ______
3. This is the more important decision. (duy nhất) → ______

C3. Viết 4 câu về gia đình/bạn bè: 2 câu comparative + 2 câu superlative.
