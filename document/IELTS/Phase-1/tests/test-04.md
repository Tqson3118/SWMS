**Q3. Chọn Simple/Continuous (10đ):**
1. Be quiet! The baby (sleeps/is sleeping).
2. My sister (is/is being) usually shy.
3. Look at him — he (cries/is crying).
4. I (don't understand/am not understanding) her.
5. We (have/are having) dinner every evening.
6. Today (is/are) Sunday.
7. (Do you cry / Are you crying) now? You look sad.
8. She (smiles/is smiling) at everyone every morning.
9. Right now they (work/are working) hard.
10. Honest people (always tell/are always telling) the truth.
