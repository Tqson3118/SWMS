## Grammar (50đ)
**Q1. Viết dạng so sánh hơn (10đ):**
tall → ___ · happy → ___ · patient → ___ · good → ___ · bad → ___ · easy → ___ · ambitious → ___ · quiet → ___ · big → ___ · confident → ___

**Q2. Điền đúng dạng (10đ):**
1. My sister is (outgoing) ______ than me.
2. This exercise is (easy) ______ than the last one.
3. He is (generous) ______ than his brother.
4. I am much (happy) ______ this year than last year.
5. Cats are (independent) ______ than dogs.

**Q3. Sửa lỗi (10đ, 2đ/câu):**
1. She is more taller than me. → ______
2. He is patienter than his father. → ______
3. This test is more easy. → ______
4. My phone is more better than yours. → ______
5. He is busier that his brother. → ______

**Q4. as...as (10đ):**
1. (tall — 2 người đều 1m80) → They are ______
2. (không giỏi bằng) → I am not ______

**Q5. more/less/fewer (10đ):**
1. We need ______ promises and ______ action.
2. I have ______ free time than last year. (time = U)
3. There were ______ people at the party. (people = C)
