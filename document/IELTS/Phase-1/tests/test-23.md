## Grammar (50đ)
**Q1. and/but/or (10đ):**
1. I want to come, ______ I'm busy. 2. Tea ______ coffee? 3. He is shy ______ very kind. 4. She opened the letter ______ started to read. 5. We can walk ______ take a taxi.

**Q2. because/so (10đ):**
1. I was tired, ______ I went to bed early. 2. I went to bed early ______ I was tired. 3. He lied, ______ she left him. 4. She left him ______ he lied. 5. It was raining, ______ we stayed home.

**Q3. although/when/if (10đ):**
1. ______ you smile, your brain feels happier. 2. He kept working ______ he was exhausted. 3. I was a child ______ I first saw the sea. 4. ______ you don't try, you will never know. 5. She stayed calm ______ she was angry inside.

**Q4. Sửa lỗi (10đ, 2đ):**
1. Because I was sick, so I stayed home. → ______
2. Although it rained, but we walked. → ______
3. She is kind and she is honest. (rút gọn nếu có thể) → ______
4. When you will come, call me. → ______
5. If you will need help, tell me. → ______

**Q5. Ghép câu (10đ):**
1. (You practice daily. Your English will improve.) if → ______
2. (It was raining. They kept walking.) although → ______
3. (I heard her voice. I felt calm.) when → ______
4. (He is 60. He runs every morning.) but → ______
5. (You can apologize. You can lose this friendship.) or → ______
