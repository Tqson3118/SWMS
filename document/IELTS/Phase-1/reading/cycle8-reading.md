# 📖 Đọc — Chu Kỳ 8 (Day 5-7): 3 bài ~110-120 từ

> Tất cả dùng phủ định + câu hỏi quá khứ (didn't/Did...?) + từ vựng cycle 8.

---

## Bài 1 — The Secret She Didn't Tell

Mai and Tuan dated for a year. They were happy — they laughed together, they traveled together, they never argued.

But Mai didn't tell Tuan one thing: she didn't feel a deep connection. He was kind, but she wasn't in love.

One evening, Tuan asked: "Do you want to meet my parents?" Mai didn't answer for a long time. Then she said the truth.

Tuan cried. He didn't shout. He didn't ask why. He just said: "Thank you for your honesty." They broke up the next week.

Honest breakups hurt less than dishonest relationships.

**Câu hỏi:**
1. Did the couple argue?
2. What didn't Mai tell Tuan?
3. How did Tuan react to the truth?

<details><summary>ĐÁP ÁN (nhấn để mở)</summary>

1. No — they never argued.
2. She didn't feel a deep connection (wasn't in love).
3. He cried but didn't shout — he thanked her for her honesty.

</details>

## Bài 2 — Did You Forgive Him?

My grandmother asked me last month: "Did you forgive your father?"

My parents divorced when I was ten. For years, I didn't speak to my father. I didn't visit. I didn't call. My anger was a wall.

"Did the wall make you happy?" my grandmother asked. I didn't answer.

Last week, I called my father. We didn't talk about the past. We talked about small things — the weather, his garden. But after the call, something heavy in my chest became lighter.

Forgiveness didn't change my father. It changed me.

**Câu hỏi:**
1. When did the parents divorce?
2. What questions did the grandmother ask?
3. Did forgiveness change the father or the writer?

<details><summary>ĐÁP ÁN (nhấn để mở)</summary>

1. When the writer was ten.
2. "Did you forgive your father?" and "Did the wall make you happy?"
3. The writer ("Forgiveness didn't change my father. It changed me.").

</details>

## Bài 3 — The Proposal

Lan's boyfriend proposed last weekend — but not the way you think.

He didn't buy a diamond ring. He didn't rent a horse carriage. He didn't even say "Will you marry me?"

What did he do? He brought a notebook. In the notebook, he wrote sixty small promises: "I promise to make coffee on sad mornings." "I promise to say sorry first." "I promise to listen without fixing." He gave her the notebook and a pen.

"Add your own promises," he said. "We're not finished. We'll never be finished."

She said yes before she finished page one.

**Câu hỏi:**
1. What DIDN'T he do (3 things)?
2. What did he bring instead?
3. Why do you think she said yes so quickly?

<details><summary>ĐÁP ÁN (nhấn để mở)</summary>

1. Didn't buy a diamond ring, didn't rent a horse carriage, didn't say "Will you marry me?" (truyền thống)
2. A notebook with sixty small promises + a pen.
3. Because the promises showed real, everyday love (giải thích tự do — chấp nhận mọi lý do hợp lý).

</details>
