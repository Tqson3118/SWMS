# 📖 Đọc — Chu Kỳ 16 (Day 13-15): should/must & trách nhiệm

> Ba bài về lời khuyên & nghĩa vụ — nhiều câu should/must/have to.

## Bài 1 — The Advice I Ignored (110 từ)

When I was 18, everyone had advice for me.

"You should study medicine," said my mother. "You must think about money," said my uncle. "You shouldn't follow your passion — passion doesn't pay bills," said a neighbor.

I ignored them all and studied art. Was I brave? Honestly, no. I was just lucky — lucky that my parents loved me more than their plans for me.

Now I'm 35, and here's my advice to young people: You shouldn't ask 'Which career is safest?' You should ask 'Which problems do I want to live with?'

Every career has problems. Money problems, people problems, meaning problems. You can't choose a life without problems. You can only choose your favorites.

**Câu hỏi:**
1. What advice did the family give at 18?
2. What questions does the writer prefer?
3. Can you choose a life without problems?

<details><summary>ĐÁP ÁN (nhấn để mở)</summary>

1. Study medicine, think about money, don't follow passion.
2. "Which problems do I want to live with?" (instead of "Which career is safest?").
3. No — you can only choose your favorite problems.

</details>

## Bài 2 — Two Letters (120 từ)

A school in Japan gave students a strange homework: write a letter to yourself at 80, and a letter from yourself at 80 to you now.

One student wrote to her future self: "You must have forgotten the exams. You must have forgotten the boy who didn't like you back. I hope you kept the small things: morning tea, grandmother's voice, Sunday walks. Don't lose those. You must protect them."

Then, writing as her 80-year-old self, she said: "Dear young me: I forgive you for worrying. None of it mattered as much as you thought. You should have slept more. You should have called your mother more. And thank you — thank you for keeping the tea, the voice, the walks. They are all I have left, and they are enough."

**Câu hỏi:**
1. What was the homework?
2. What must the 80-year-old self protect?
3. What should the young self have done more (2 things)?

<details><summary>ĐÁP ÁN (nhấn để mở)</summary>

1. Write a letter TO yourself at 80, and a letter FROM yourself at 80 back to you now.
2. The small things: morning tea, grandmother's voice, Sunday walks.
3. Slept more, called her mother more.

</details>

## Bài 3 — Rules of the House (110 từ)

In our house, we have rules. Some are musts, some are shoulds.

You must be honest. Lies are not allowed in this house — even small ones, even kind ones. You must respect closed doors.

You should say sorry quickly — the same day, not next week. You should knock before entering. You should ask "How was your day?" and really listen to the answer.

And here are the don'ts: You mustn't use words as weapons. You mustn't bring up old fights in new fights.

But you don't have to be perfect. You don't have to be in a good mood. You don't even have to talk — silence together counts as together.

My daughter will leave this house one day. My deepest hope: she takes the rules with her, and builds a kinder house of her own.

**Câu hỏi:**
1. What are the two MUST rules?
2. What are the mustn'ts?
3. What don't you have to do in this house?

<details><summary>ĐÁP ÁN (nhấn để mở)</summary>

1. Be honest (no lies, even kind ones) and respect closed doors.
2. Use words as weapons; bring up old fights in new fights.
3. Be perfect, be in a good mood, or even talk — silence together counts.

</details>
