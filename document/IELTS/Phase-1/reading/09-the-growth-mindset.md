# 📖 Đọc 9 — The Growth Mindset (130 từ)

> Khái niệm nổi tiếng của Carol Dweck (đơn giản hóa) — bài đọc khép chu kỳ 13.

---

Psychologist Carol Dweck found two types of students.

Students with a fixed mindset believe: "I am born smart or stupid. Talent is everything. If I fail, I am a failure." They avoid hard tests because failure hurts their identity.

Students with a growth mindset believe: "My brain can grow like a muscle. Effort matters more than talent. If I fail, I can learn." They choose hard tests because challenges make them stronger.

The most interesting discovery: mindset can change. When students learned the word "YET," everything shifted. Not "I can't do this" — but "I can't do this YET."

One word. Three letters. A different life.

You are learning English. Some days are hard. On those days, remember: you are not slow. You are not untalented. You are simply early in the journey — and the journey is long because it is worth it.
