# 📖 Đọc 10 — The Silent Language (110 từ)

> Tháng 4 mở màn — gạch chân các từ body language đã học hôm nay (gesture, posture, eye contact...).

---

Words are only one part of interpersonal communication. Psychological research suggests that a significant amount of emotional meaning travels through non-verbal channels — gestures, posture, facial expressions, and vocal tone.

Think about everyday situations. When someone says "I'm fine" with tense shoulders and weary eyes, do you only listen to the words? Non-verbal cues often provide valuable clues about genuine feelings. While spoken words can be carefully managed, physiological tension often reflects underlying stress or hesitation.

Studies in communication highlight an important tendency: when verbal and non-verbal signals conflict, people naturally look to non-verbal cues for deeper context. Psychologists refer to this contrast as incongruence — a perceptible gap between what someone says and how they behave. However, body language is not a foolproof lie detector; tight shoulders might simply reflect cold weather or fatigue. Effective observers treat gestures as helpful clues to check against a person's baseline, rather than jumping to absolute conclusions.

So observe the silent language with patience. Notice the hands. Observe the posture. Listen to the tone, alongside the words. Non-verbal cues offer an invitation to greater empathy — when you pay thoughtful attention.
