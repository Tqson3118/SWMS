## Bài 1 — Feeling Good on a Bad Day (ngày D5)

Nam is a hard-working student. He studies every day, but today he feels stressed. He has three tests this week, and his brain is overwhelmed.

His friend Linh sees this. She is a calm and caring person. "Nam," she says, "you look tired. Take a break with me."

They walk in the park. They laugh at funny videos. Slowly, Nam feels relaxed. "Thank you," he says. "I feel better now."

A good friend is medicine for a stressed heart.

**Câu hỏi:**
1. Why does Nam feel stressed?
2. What does Linh do to help?
3. Tìm trong bài 3 tính từ mô tả Linh.

<details><summary>ĐÁP ÁN (nhấn để mở — chỉ mở sau khi làm)</summary>

1. He has three tests this week and feels overwhelmed.
2. She walks with him in the park and they laugh together.
3. calm, caring (và tốt: friendly-style gợi ý) — chấp nhận calm/caring + relaxed nếu diễn giải được.

</details>

## Bài 2 — The Moody Brother (ngày D6)

My younger brother is a moody person. In the morning, he is cheerful and friendly. In the afternoon, he is upset about small things. My mother never gets mad at him. She is patient.

"Feelings are like weather," she says. "A moody day is not a bad person."

Now, when my brother is upset, I don't argue. I stay calm. I ask: "Do you want to talk, or do you want quiet?" Usually, he wants quiet first, and then he talks.

**Câu hỏi:**
1. Is the brother cheerful in the morning or afternoon?
2. What does the mother compare feelings to?
3. What does the writer ask the brother when he is upset?

<details><summary>ĐÁP ÁN (nhấn để mở — chỉ mở sau khi làm)</summary>

1. In the morning.
2. Weather (feelings are like weather).
3. "Do you want to talk, or do you want quiet?"

</details>

## Bài 3 — Quiet People (ngày D7)

Some people think quiet people are boring. This is not true.

Quiet people are often interested in deep things — books, feelings, ideas. They listen more than they talk. When they talk, their words are careful and wise.

My aunt is a quiet person. At family parties, she doesn't laugh loudly. But when I have a problem, she is the first person I call. She listens. She thinks. Then she gives wise advice.

Quiet is not empty. Quiet is full.

**Câu hỏi:**
1. Are quiet people boring, according to the text?
2. Why does the writer call their aunt first when there's a problem?
3. "Quiet is not empty. Quiet is ___" — từ nào tổng hợp ý bài?

<details><summary>ĐÁP ÁN (nhấn để mở — chỉ mở sau khi làm)</summary>

1. No — they are often interested in deep things.
2. Because she listens, thinks, and gives wise advice.
3. full.

</details>
