# 📖 Đọc 4 — Falling in Love (100 từ)

> Tháng 2 mở màn: bài đọc FULL Past Simple — gạch chân tất cả động từ quá khứ bạn tìm thấy.

---

Nobody plans to fall in love. It just happens.

Last spring, Nam met Linh at a friend's wedding. They talked for two hours about books and music. He felt something strange in his heart. She gave him her phone number on a small piece of paper.

They went on a first date the next week. He arrived early. She arrived early too — they laughed about it. They walked in the park, and he held her hand for the first time.

Six months later, he proposed. She said yes. Their friends weren't surprised.

---

## Câu hỏi

1. Where and when did Nam meet Linh?
2. What did she give him?
3. Were their friends surprised at the end? Why do you think so?

---

**ĐÁP ÁN:**
1. At a friend's wedding, last spring.
2. Her phone number (on a small piece of paper).
3. No — because their love grew naturally and visibly ("weren't surprised").

> Gạch chân được: met, talked, felt, gave, went, arrived, laughed, walked, held, proposed, said, weren't — 12+ động từ quá khứ!
