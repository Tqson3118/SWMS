## Bài 1 — Feelings Come and Go (ngày D17)

This morning, I woke up in a bad mood. Everything looked grey. My coffee tasted bitter. My room seemed small.

Then I remembered: feelings come and go. This mood is a cloud, not the sky. I am not my anger. I am not my sadness. I am the person who watches them come and go.

By noon, the cloud moved. The same coffee tasted fine. The same room seemed warm. Nothing outside changed — only my inside.

When a feeling visits you, be polite. But remember: you are the house, not the weather.

**Câu hỏi:**
1. How did things look in the morning?
2. What is the difference between the cloud and the sky?
3. "You are the ___, not the ___" — điền.

<details><summary>ĐÁP ÁN (nhấn để mở — chỉ mở sau khi làm)</summary>

1. Grey (coffee bitter, room small).
2. The mood/feeling is the cloud; I am the sky — feelings pass, I remain.
3. house / weather.

</details>

## Bài 2 — How to Calm Down (ngày D18)

When panic comes, your body speaks first: the heart beats fast, the breath becomes short, the hands grow cold. Your mind is not broken — your body is just ringing an old alarm.

Here is a simple recipe. One: breathe in for four seconds. Two: hold for four seconds. Three: breathe out slowly for eight seconds. Repeat five times.

Then name the feeling: "This is anxiety. It is uncomfortable, but it is not dangerous."

A feeling you can name is a feeling you can hold. Stay calm, breathe, and let the alarm ring — it will stop.

**Câu hỏi:**
1. What does the body do when panic comes (3 signs)?
2. What are the three steps of the breathing recipe?
3. Why should you name the feeling?

<details><summary>ĐÁP ÁN (nhấn để mở — chỉ mở sau khi làm)</summary>

1. Heart beats fast, breath becomes short, hands grow cold.
2. In 4s → hold 4s → out 8s (repeat 5 times).
3. Because a feeling you can name is a feeling you can hold.

</details>

## Bài 3 — The Two Islands (review) (ngày D19)

Imagine two islands. On Island One, people hide their feelings. A man is sad, but he says "I'm fine." A woman is lonely, but she smiles all day. Their feelings go underground and become heavy stones.

On Island Two, people welcome their feelings. When someone cries, a friend sits near and says: "Take your time." When someone is angry, they ask: "What do you need?"

Which island has more laughter? The second one. People who can feel sadness deeply can also feel joy deeply. The heart is one muscle — you cannot numb one feeling and keep the others.

**Câu hỏi:**
1. What happens to hidden feelings on Island One?
2. What do friends say when someone cries on Island Two?
3. Why does Island Two have more laughter?

<details><summary>ĐÁP ÁN (nhấn để mở — chỉ mở sau khi làm)</summary>

1. They go underground and become heavy stones.
2. "Take your time."
3. Because people who feel sadness deeply also feel joy deeply — the heart is one muscle.

</details>
