## Bài 1 — The Question I Ask Myself (100 từ)

Every December, I ask myself one question: "What would I do if I weren't afraid?"

Last year, the answer was: "I would start my own business." So I did. It might fail. It will probably be hard. But the alternative — never knowing — is worse.

Two years ago, the answer was: "I would tell her how I feel." I didn't. She moved away. It wasn't a tragedy, but it was a lesson.

Fear says: "Wait. Maybe later. What if you fail?" Courage says: "What if it works? What if it changes everything?"

You might fail. That's true. You also might fly. The question is which truth you choose to live by.

**Câu hỏi:**
1. What question does the writer ask every December?
2. What did they NOT do two years ago?
3. What does fear say, and what does courage say?

1. "What would I do if I weren't afraid?"
2. Tell her how they feel (she moved away).
3. Fear: "Wait. Maybe later. What if you fail?" — Courage: "What if it works? What if it changes everything?"

## Bài 2 — Possibility & Probability (110 từ)

Two words live in the future: possibility and probability.

Probability asks: "What will probably happen?" If you play the lottery, you will probably lose. That's probability.

Possibility asks: "What could happen?" Someone wins the lottery every week. That's possibility.

Wise people respect probability. They wear seatbelts. They save money. They don't build their lives on lightning strikes.

But wise people also feed possibility. They send the application that will probably be rejected — because it might not be. They take the meeting that probably goes nowhere — because it might go everywhere.

Live on probability: build your floor with realistic plans. But leave a window open for possibility: take small chances regularly. The floor keeps you safe. The window keeps you alive.

**Câu hỏi:**
1. What's the difference between probability and possibility?
2. How do wise people respect probability (2 examples)?
3. What do the floor and the window mean?

<details><summary>ĐÁP ÁN (nhấn để mở)</summary>

1. Probability = what will probably happen (likely); possibility = what could happen (maybe).
2. Wear seatbelts, save money, don't build life on lightning strikes (any 2).
3. The floor = safety (realistic plans); the window = aliveness (small chances).

</details>

## Bài 3 — Who Could You Become? (110 từ)

Here's an experiment. Complete this sentence: "I could become someone who ______."

Maybe: "...who runs every morning." "...who speaks three languages." "...who says sorry first." "...who reads 20 books a year."

Now the strange part: that person — the runner, the speaker, the reader — is not a different person. It's you, plus a system, plus time.

A year is 365 chances to practice. The person you could become is built one ordinary Tuesday at a time: one vocabulary deck, one walk, one page, one honest conversation.

Nobody transforms overnight. But everybody transforms over hundreds of nights.

Write your sentence. Then make this Tuesday count.
