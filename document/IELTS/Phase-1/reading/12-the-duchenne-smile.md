# 📖 Đọc 12 — The Duchenne Smile (120 từ)

> Nụ cười thật vs nụ cười giả — khép chu kỳ 19.

---

In the 1860s, French scientist Duchenne discovered something interesting: there are two kinds of smiles.

The first kind uses only the mouth. The lips turn up, but the eyes stay flat. This is the polite smile — the one we give to cameras and strangers. Scientists call it the fake smile.

The second kind uses the mouth AND the eyes. The corners of the eyes wrinkle — small lines appear. This smile is warm, genuine, involuntary. Your face makes it automatically when you feel real joy. This is the Duchenne smile.

Here is why it matters: you cannot fake a Duchenne smile. Try it in a mirror. You can raise your lips easily, but you cannot command the muscles around your eyes. They only move for true happiness.

So next time someone smiles at you, look at their eyes, not their mouth. The mouth can lie. The eyes are honest guests at every true party.

---

## Câu hỏi

1. What is the difference between the two kinds of smiles?
