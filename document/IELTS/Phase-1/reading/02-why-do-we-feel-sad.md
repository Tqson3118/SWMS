# 📖 Đọc 2 — Why Do We Feel Sad? (90 từ)

> Chủ đề: nỗi buồn là thông điệp, không phải kẻ thù.

---

Why do we feel sad? Sadness is not a problem. Sadness is a message.

When we lose something — a friend, a dream, a happy time — we feel sad. The tear says: "This was important to me." A person without sadness never loves anything deeply.

So don't run away from sad feelings. Sit with them. Ask: "What is this feeling teaching me?" Then, one morning, the heart is light again. Sadness comes, and sadness goes. Love stays.
