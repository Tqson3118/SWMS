## Bài 1 — The Broken Promise (ngày D9)

Hoa is disappointed today. Her best friend Minh promised to help her study, but he forgot. Now Hoa is upset and a little angry.

"Are you going to shout at him?" I ask.

"No," Hoa says. "I am angry, but I am not mad at him — I am mad at the situation. Minh is reliable most of the time. Everyone forgets sometimes."

Hoa feels her anger, then lets it go. This is emotional intelligence: feel the feeling, but choose your answer.

**Câu hỏi:**
1. Why is Hoa disappointed?
2. Is Hoa mad at Minh or at the situation?
3. What is "emotional intelligence" in this story?

<details><summary>ĐÁP ÁN (nhấn để mở — chỉ mở sau khi làm)</summary>

1. Minh promised to help her study but forgot.
2. At the situation.
3. Feel the feeling, but choose your answer (không để cảm xúc quyết định hành vi).

</details>

## Bài 2 — The Rude Person on the Bus (ngày D10)

Yesterday, an old man sat next to a young man on the bus. The young man looked at his phone the whole time. He didn't say "hello" or give his seat. A woman behind them whispered: "So rude!"

But maybe the story is bigger. Maybe the young man was tired. Maybe he was sad about something. Maybe his mother was sick.

We see people's behavior, but we never see their whole story. Before you call someone rude, remember: you don't know their day.

**Câu hỏi:**
1. What did the young man do (or not do)?
2. Give two possible reasons for his behavior.
3. "We see people's ___, but we never see their ___" — điền.

<details><summary>ĐÁP ÁN (nhấn để mở — chỉ mở sau khi làm)</summary>

1. He looked at his phone, didn't greet or give his seat.
2. He was tired / sad / his mother was sick (bất kỳ 2 lý do hợp lý).
3. behavior / whole story.

</details>

## Bài 3 — Who Am I? (Personality) (ngày D11)

Psychologists say: personality is like a garden. Some parts grow with you from birth — some children are brave, some are shy. But the garden changes. A shy child can become a confident adult with practice. A selfish teen can become a generous father after he has his own children.

Don't say: "I am like this, I cannot change." Say: "This is my garden today. What will I water tomorrow?"

You are not a stone. You are a garden.

**Câu hỏi:**
1. What is personality compared to?
2. Can a shy child become confident? What is needed?
3. "You are not a stone. You are a ___" — điền.

<details><summary>ĐÁP ÁN (nhấn để mở — chỉ mở sau khi làm)</summary>

1. A garden.
2. Yes — with practice.
3. garden.

</details>
