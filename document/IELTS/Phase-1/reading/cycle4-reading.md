## Bài 1 — The Vocabulary of the Heart (ngày D13)

A rich emotional vocabulary is a gift. Some people know only two words: "good" and "bad". Their world is black and white.

But look at your vocabulary now: joy, sorrow, anger, fear, shame, guilt, pride, relief, envy, courage... With these words, your inner world becomes colorful. You are not just "in a bad mood" — you are disappointed, or lonely, or overwhelmed. Each word shows you a different door out.

When you name a feeling correctly, the feeling becomes smaller and lighter.

**Câu hỏi:**
1. What do people with only "good" and "bad" miss?
2. Name four emotion nouns from the text.
3. What happens when you name a feeling correctly?

<details><summary>ĐÁP ÁN (nhấn để mở — chỉ mở sau khi làm)</summary>

1. The color/detail of their inner world (they see black and white).
2. Bất kỳ 4: joy, sorrow, anger, fear, shame, guilt, pride, relief, envy, courage.
3. It becomes smaller and lighter.

</details>

## Bài 2 — Guilt and Shame (ngày D14)

Guilt says: "I did something bad." Shame says: "I am bad." They feel similar, but they are very different.

Guilt is useful. It moves us to say sorry and repair the mistake. A person who feels no guilt makes the same mistake again and again.

Shame is heavier. Shame hides. It makes us small and quiet. Psychology says: listen to your guilt, but question your shame. You make mistakes — but you are not a mistake.

**Câu hỏi:**
1. What does guilt say? What does shame say?
2. Which feeling is useful, and why?
3. "You make mistakes — but you are ___" — điền.

<details><summary>ĐÁP ÁN (nhấn để mở — chỉ mở sau khi làm)</summary>

1. Guilt: "I did something bad." Shame: "I am bad."
2. Guilt — it moves us to say sorry and repair mistakes.
3. not a mistake.

</details>

## Bài 3 — Courage Is Not Loud (ngày D15)

People think courage is loud: a soldier, a fireman, a hero. But most courage is quiet.

Courage is the shy student who raises her hand. Courage is the man who says "I am sorry" first after a fight. Courage is asking for help when you are helpless. Courage is feeling the fear and doing the thing anyway.

Every day, ordinary people are being brave in ordinary ways. Maybe you are being brave today — just by studying English.

**Câu hỏi:**
1. Is courage always loud, according to the text?
2. Give two examples of quiet courage.
3. What is the last example of courage in the text?

<details><summary>ĐÁP ÁN (nhấn để mở — chỉ mở sau khi làm)</summary>

1. No — most courage is quiet.
2. The shy student raising her hand; saying sorry first; asking for help (bất kỳ 2).
3. Studying English (you, the reader, being brave today).

</details>
