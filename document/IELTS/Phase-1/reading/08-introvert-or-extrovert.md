# 📖 Đọc 8 — Introvert or Extrovert? (120 từ)

> Hai loại người nổi tiếng nhất trong tâm lý học tính cách — đọc và tự nhận diện mình.

---

Are you an introvert or an extrovert?

Extroverts get energy from people. After a party, they feel alive. They think by talking. They are usually louder and more outgoing.

Introverts get energy from silence. After a party, they need a quiet room to recharge. They think by thinking. They are usually quieter and more reflective.

But here is the secret: most people are ambiverts — a little of both. A shy person can be an extrovert at home. A loud person can be an introvert inside.

The biggest mistake is thinking one type is better. The world needs quiet thinkers and loud doers. The world needs listeners and talkers. Your type is not your limit — it is your starting point.

Which one are you? Maybe the most honest answer is: "It depends on the day."
