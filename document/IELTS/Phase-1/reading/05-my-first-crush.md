# 📖 Đọc 5 — My First Crush (110 từ)

> Cảm nắng tuổi học trò — past simple xen a few/a little (chuẩn bị cho grammar 8).

---

I was fourteen. She sat next to me in English class — of all subjects!

Her name was Ha. She had a quiet smile and a few freckles. I thought about her every day, but I never said a word. I was too shy.

For two years, I watched her from a distance. I bought her a small gift for her birthday, but I left it in my bag. I practiced conversations with her in my head a hundred times.

Then her family moved to another city. I never confessed my feelings.

That was twenty years ago. I still remember her sometimes — not with sadness, but with a small smile. First crushes never really leave us.

---

## Câu hỏi

1. How old was the writer? Where did Ha sit?
2. Did the writer ever confess? Why not?
3. "First crushes never really ___ us" — điền từ.

---

**ĐÁP ÁN:**
1. Fourteen. Next to the writer in English class.
2. No — too shy (practiced conversations in his head but never spoke).
3. leave.

> Lưu ý: *a few freckles* (C) vs *too shy* — bài này có sẵn mầm cho much/many.
