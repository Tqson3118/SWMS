# 📖 Đọc 6 — Why Couples Argue (120 từ)

> Kết chu kỳ 7 — tâm lý học thật về tranh cãi (nghiên cứu Gottman đơn giản hóa).

---

Psychologist John Gottman watched thousands of couples for forty years. He found something interesting: all couples argue. Happy couples argue. Unhappy couples argue. The difference is not the fighting — it is the way couples fight and repair.

Gottman saw four bad habits. He called them the Four Horsemen: criticism ("You always..."), contempt (eye-rolling, sarcasm), defensiveness ("It's not my fault!"), and stonewalling (the silent wall).

But he also found a magic number: happy couples have five positive moments for every negative moment. A small kindness, a touch, a joke — these repair the small breaks of daily life.

So don't dream of a relationship without arguments. Dream of a relationship that argues well — and repairs well.
