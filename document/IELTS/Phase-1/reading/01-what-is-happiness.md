# 📖 Đọc 1 — What Is Happiness? (80 từ)

> Đọc 2 lần: lần 1 trôi chảy không dịch, lần 2 đọc to + gạch từ mới. Sau đó trả lời 3 câu hỏi (20-25'). Rồi chuyển sang graded reader 20'.

---

What is happiness? Happiness is not a big thing. It is a warm feeling inside.

For some people, happiness is a quiet morning with a cup of tea. For others, it is laughter with friends. A baby feels happiness when her mother smiles. A student feels happiness after a good test.

Money can buy many things, but it cannot buy happiness. Happiness grows when we share it. Give your happiness to another person, and you have more, not less.

---

## Câu hỏi

1. Is happiness a big thing or small warm feeling?
2. Give TWO examples of happiness in the text.
3. What happens to happiness when we share it?

---

**ĐÁP ÁN:**
1. It is a small warm feeling inside (not a big thing).
2. Ví dụ: quiet morning with tea / laughter with friends / mother's smile / good test result.
3. It grows — you have more, not less.
