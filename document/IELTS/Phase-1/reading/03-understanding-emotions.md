# 📖 Đọc 3 — Understanding Emotions (100 từ)

> Bài đọc khép chu kỳ 1 — tổng hợp cả 24 từ cảm xúc.

---

Emotions are colors of the heart. Happy is yellow. Angry is red. Sad is blue. Jealous is green — people even say "green with envy".

Every emotion has a job. Fear says: "Be careful!" Anger says: "Something is wrong!" Guilt says: "Say sorry." Joy says: "This is good — do it again!"

Some people hide their emotions. But hidden emotions do not die; they sleep. One day, a small problem wakes them, and they come out — big and loud.

So listen to your emotions. They are not your boss, but they are your friends.

---

## Câu hỏi

1. What color is jealousy, according to the text?
2. What is the job of fear? Of guilt?
3. What happens to hidden emotions?

---

**ĐÁP ÁN:**
1. Green ("green with envy").
2. Fear says "Be careful!" — guilt says "Say sorry."
3. They do not die — they sleep, then wake up big and loud.
