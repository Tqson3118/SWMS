## Bài 1 — The Teacher of Love (bài mới, 150 từ)

My grandmother never read a book about relationships. But she taught me more about love than any book.

When my grandfather was angry, she didn't argue back. She waited an hour, then she brought him tea. "Anger is a storm," she said. "You don't fight a storm. You close the windows and wait."

When they had a disagreement, she made a rule: one speaks, one listens, then change. "The ear is more important than the mouth," she said. "God gave you two ears and one mouth — that is the correct order."

For sixty years, they kept small habits: she made his coffee. He warmed her side of the bed. They never said "I love you" loudly — they said it in coffee and warm beds.

She told me once: "Love is not a feeling, my child. A feeling comes and goes. Love is a practice — like brushing your teeth. You do it every day, especially on the days you don't feel like it."

**Câu hỏi:**
1. What did she do when grandfather was angry?
2. What was her listening rule?
3. How did they say "I love you" for sixty years?
4. Tìm 1 câu dùng Past Simple + didn't, và 1 câu dùng mạo từ a/an.

1. She waited an hour, then brought him tea.
2. One speaks, one listens, then change (đổi vai).
