## Bài 1 — The Mirror (bài mới, 150 từ)

A king kept a magic mirror that showed people their true selves.

A proud soldier looked in and saw a frightened boy. A quiet servant looked in and saw a radiant queen. A rich merchant saw a beggar counting coins he didn't need. An old woman saw a young girl dancing.

The king was confused. "The mirror lies!" he shouted.

An old servant said gently: "Your Majesty, the mirror doesn't lie. It shows what psychologists would call the inner self — who we are beneath the surface. The soldier hides fear behind bravery. The servant hides royalty behind modesty. The merchant hides emptiness behind wealth. The woman keeps her young heart."

"And what would I see?" asked the king.

"That," smiled the servant, "you must discover yourself. The mirror can only show you the door. You must be brave enough to walk through."

Knowing yourself is the beginning of wisdom. It might also be the hardest journey you'll ever take — because you can't run away from the one who travels with you.

**Câu hỏi:**
1. What did each person see in the mirror? (4 người)
2. What does the mirror really show?
3. Why is knowing yourself the hardest journey?

<details><summary>ĐÁP ÁN (nhấn để mở)</summary>

1. Soldier → frightened boy; servant → radiant queen; merchant → beggar; old woman → young girl dancing.
2. The inner self — who we are beneath the surface.
3. Because you can't run away from the one who travels with you (yourself).

</details>
