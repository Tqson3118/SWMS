## Bài 1 — The Teacher of Feelings (bài mới, 150 từ) (ngày D23)

My grandmother never studied psychology. But she understands feelings better than any book.

When I was a child and I cried, she never said "Don't cry." She sat with me and waited. When the tears finished, she asked one question: "What does your heart say?"

When I was angry, she gave me work to do with my hands — washing rice, watering plants. "Anger is energy," she said. "Use it, don't throw it at people."

When I was jealous of my cousin, she laughed kindly: "Good! Jealousy is showing you what you want. Now go and work for it."

I am not a child now. But when feelings confuse me, I still hear her questions. A wise person does not need big words. A wise person asks the right question.

**Câu hỏi:**
1. What did the grandmother do when the writer cried?
2. What did she say about anger?
3. What does jealousy show us, according to her?
4. Tìm 1 câu dùng Present Continuous và 1 câu dùng trạng từ tần suất trong bài.

<details><summary>ĐÁP ÁN (nhấn để mở — chỉ mở sau khi làm)</summary>

1. She sat with the writer and waited (never said "Don't cry").
2. "Anger is energy — use it, don't throw it at people."
3. It shows what you want — go and work for it.
4. Continuous: "she is showing you" (trong câu trích dẫn trực tiếp) — hoặc câu cuối "I still hear her questions" không phải continuous; chấp nhận: "jealousy is showing you". Tần suất: "never said", "never studied", "still hear" — bất kỳ 1 câu đúng.

</details>
