## Bài 1 — The Body Never Lies — bài cuối Phase 1 (150 từ)

Four months ago, you knew one hundred English words. Today, you are reading this paragraph — a paragraph about psychology, written in real English. Stop for one second and feel that.

This text is about body language, but it is really about you. When you started, your posture toward English was closed: tense shoulders, worried eyes, the body language of "I can't." Look at yourself now. You lean in. You make eye contact with the page. Your hands turn the pages without fear.

People said learning a language is about grammar and vocabulary. They are only half right, because the other half is posture — the posture of the heart. Some students know 500 words and quit, because their hearts say "too late." Other students know 300 words and continue, because their hearts say "not yet" — and they walk on.

You have now finished the foundation. Phase 2 will be harder, but difficulty is just growth wearing work clothes.

So stand up straight. Shoulders back. Eyes forward. That is not just body language — that is a prophecy. Your body already knows: you are going to make it.

**Câu hỏi:**
1. How has the reader's 'posture toward English' changed, according to the text?
2. What is the 'other half' of learning a language?
3. What does the final paragraph call standing up straight?

<details><summary>ĐÁP ÁN (nhấn để mở)</summary>

1. From closed (tense, worried, "I can't") to open (leaning in, eye contact, no fear).
2. The posture of the heart — what your heart says ("too late" vs "not yet").
3. A prophecy — your body already knows you are going to make it.

</details>
