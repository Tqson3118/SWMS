# 📖 Đọc 7 — Who Am I? (110 từ)

> Tháng 3 mở màn — tự nhận thức. Gạch chân mọi câu có so sánh hơn (-er/more...than).

---

Who am I? This is the biggest question in psychology.

I am taller than my brother, but he is stronger than me. I am more patient than I look. I am quieter than my best friend, but louder than my mother. I am as stubborn as my grandmother — she says it with pride.

But I am more than a list of adjectives. I am my choices. I am what I do on difficult days. I am the person I am becoming.

Psychologists say: personality is not a box; it is a river. Today's you is not the final you. You are more adaptable than you think.

So ask the question again every year: Who am I? The answer will be richer each time.

---

## Câu hỏi

1. Find THREE comparisons in the text (write them).
2. What is personality compared to? Why?
3. "Today's you is not ___ ___ ___" — điền.

---

**ĐÁP ÁN:**
1. Ví dụ: taller than my brother · stronger than me · more patient than I look · quieter than my best friend · louder than my mother · as stubborn as my grandmother · more adaptable than you think (chọn 3). 
2. A river — because it moves and changes; personality is not fixed (a box).
3. the final you.
