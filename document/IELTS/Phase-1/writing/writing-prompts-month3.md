## Checklist tự sửa (tháng 3)

1. Comparative: -er/more + **than** đủ chưa?
2. Superlative: có **the** + -est/most chưa?
3. Modal + V nguyên mẫu (can speak — không to, không -s)?
4. mustn't (cấm) vs don't have to (không cần) — đúng ý chưa?
5. Bất quy tắc: good-better-best, bad-worse-worst?

## Prompt chấm bài viết cuối tuần

```text
You are my English teacher. Check this text about personality. Focus on:
1) comparative/superlative forms, 2) modal verbs (can/should/must/would + base verb),
3) mustn't vs don't have to.
Correct ALL mistakes and explain each briefly in Vietnamese. Then rewrite my text
in better English (still simple A2 level). Give me a score 0-10.
My text: [dán bài viết]
```
