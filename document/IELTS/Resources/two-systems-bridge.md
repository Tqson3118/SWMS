# Two systems: a short reading bridge

Đọc trước bài nghe Day25; đây là bài đọc, không có audio.

## Source-based explanation

Kahneman's account describes two kinds of processing. System 1 supplies quick associations with little deliberate effort. System 2 uses attention to work through rules and can check an initial impression. That checking may be weak. A quick judgment can be useful; a slower judgment can still be wrong. These labels describe processing, rather than two people or guaranteed levels of accuracy.

The adapted TED listening task has a different objective. Completing it does not complete this reading objective.

## Authored practice

Mina quickly guesses a total. She then adds each item and checks her arithmetic.

1. Which action illustrates each mode?
2. Does careful addition guarantee the correct answer? Explain.

<details>
<summary>Đáp án — mở sau khi làm</summary>

1. Quick guessing illustrates System 1; deliberate checking illustrates System 2.
2. No. She can misread an item or calculate incorrectly.

</details>
