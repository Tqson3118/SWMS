# Grammar 07 — Reporting verbs and complement patterns

> Ba buổi học độc lập, mỗi buổi 60 phút. Bài luyện chọn reporting-verb pattern trong tình huống hư cấu; nội dung chỉ giới hạn ở các mẫu reporting verb.

## Cách dùng trong ba buổi

Mỗi buổi: **10 phút nhận diện → 20 phút hoàn thành → 20 phút viết mới → 5 phút tự chấm → 5 phút lập lịch ôn**. Làm bảng trống trước, rồi mới mở **Đáp án ẩn**. Mỗi đáp án dùng mã quy tắc; câu thay thế được nhận khi giữ mẫu, nghĩa và bối cảnh.

## Quy tắc nguồn ngắn gọn

- **R1 — direct infinitive:** [agree](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/agree) shows *agree to do something* (say-yes sense, body 187–196); [decide](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/decide) shows *decide to do something* (sense 1, body 124–131); [offer](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/offer_1) shows *offer to do something* (sense 2, body 173–177); [promise](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/promise_1) shows *promise to do something* and the attested negative example *promise not to* (sense 1, body 114–119); [refuse](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/refuse1) shows *refuse to do something* (body 113–119).
- **R2 — recipient before infinitive:** [encourage](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/encourage) shows *encourage somebody to do something* (body 142–145); [invite](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/invite_1) shows *invite somebody to do something* (sense 1, body 114–122); [persuade](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/persuade) shows *persuade somebody to do something* (body 113–115); [remind](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/remind) shows *remind somebody to do something* (body 113–121); [warn](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/warn) shows *warn somebody to do something* (body 184–190); [warn](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/warn) also shows *warn somebody not to do something* (body 184–190).
- **R3 — direct -ing:** Oxford [admit](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/admit) shows *admit doing something*; [deny](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/deny) shows *deny doing something*; [mention](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/mention_1) shows *mention doing something*; [recommend](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/recommend) shows *recommend doing something*; and [suggest](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/suggest_1) shows *suggest doing something*. These are five separate direct *verb-ing* constructions.
- **R4 — dependent preposition:** The British Council C1 source gives *apologise for not being present* and the readable explanation gives this *complain about receiving important information*. The explanation says dependent-preposition verbs take *-ing* after the preposition; its named list includes this pair. Oxford [insist](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/insist) shows *insist on somebody/something doing something*, and Oxford [object](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/object_2) shows *object to doing something*. In *object to*, *to* is a preposition.
- **R5 — object + preposition:** Oxford [congratulate](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/congratulate) shows *congratulate somebody (on something)*; the British Council C1 object-plus-preposition section lists *congratulate (someone on)*. The ledger maps this named list to the ID 18 object slot;  The authored teaching restatement is *congratulate somebody on doing something*, used in ID 18.
- **R6 — that + base:** The [suggest](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/suggest_1), [demand](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/demand_2), and [insist](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/insist) entries separately support *that + subject + base verb* and their named British-English *should + verb* alternatives.
- **R7 — continuing truth:** EnglishClub’s [reported-statements page](https://www.englishclub.com/grammar/sentence/reported-statements.php), body 23–26, 42–48, and 50–56, distinguishes a past reporting frame from a statement that remains current. Use the past form for the earlier frame and retain the present when the stated fact continues.
- **R8 — object + that:** The [assure](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/assure), [remind](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/remind), and [warn](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/warn) entries separately support object before a *that*-clause; choose tense from the time and truth condition.

Đọc thêm: [Oxford — suggest](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/suggest_1), [Oxford — demand](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/demand_2), [Oxford — insist](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/insist), [Oxford — assure](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/assure), [Oxford — remind](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/remind), [Oxford — warn](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/warn), [British Council — Patterns with reporting verbs](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/c1/patterns-reporting-verbs), and [EnglishClub — Reported statements](https://www.englishclub.com/grammar/sentence/reported-statements.php). Ví dụ và model dưới đây là hư cấu, do bài viết mới.

## Buổi 1 — Infinitive patterns (60 phút)

### Nhận diện và hoàn thành

Điền phần còn thiếu. Viết toàn bộ pattern.

| # | Bối cảnh hư cấu | Gợi ý | Quy tắc |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mai agreed ___ the inventory after lunch. | `agree to do`: no object before *to* | R1 |
| 2 | The curator decided ___ the fragile model indoors. | `decide to do`: no object before *to* | R1 |
| 3 | The coach offered ___ the rehearsal notes. | `offer to do`: the subject offers their action | R1 |
| 4 | The editor encouraged Lan ___ the title once more. | `encourage + object + to` | R2 |
| 5 | The guide reminded the visitors ___ their badges. | `remind + object + to` | R2 |
| 6 | The panel persuaded Minh ___ the quieter route. | `persuade + object + to` | R2 |
| 7 | The notice warned residents ___ near the wet paint. | `warn + object + not to` | R2 |
| 8 | The host invited the finalists ___ their prototypes. | `invite + object + to` | R2 |
| 9 | The sponsor refused ___ the draft before Friday. | `refuse to do`: no object before *to* | R1 |

<details><summary>Đáp án ẩn và lý do</summary>

1 **agreed to check**; 2 **decided to keep**; 3 **offered to share**; 4 **encouraged Lan to revise**; 5 **reminded the visitors to wear**; 6 **persuaded Minh to choose**; 7 **warned residents not to stand**; 8 **invited the finalists to present**; 9 **refused to release**. R1: **agree** direct *to do*; **decide** direct *to do*; **offer** direct *to do*; **refuse** direct *to do*. R2: **encourage** recipient + *to*; **remind** recipient + *to*; **persuade** recipient + *to*; **invite** recipient + *to*; **warn** recipient + *to*; **warn** recipient + *not to*.

</details>

### Tạo câu mới

Viết 5 câu cho một nhóm hư cấu chuẩn bị triển lãm đêm: ít nhất hai câu R1 và ba câu R2. Tự chấm 1 điểm mỗi tiêu chí: reporting verb, object, infinitive, ngữ cảnh; 14/20 là mốc tự luyện. Model chỉ mở sau khi làm:

<details><summary>Model key (5 câu hư cấu, tùy chọn)</summary>

1. The night team agreed to label the lanterns.
2. The curator decided to lock the side door.
3. The stage manager offered to test the projector.
4. The editor encouraged Linh to revise the poster.
5. The guide reminded the visitors to carry a pass.

</details>

## Buổi 2 — -ing and preposition patterns (60 phút)

### Nhận diện và hoàn thành

| # | Bối cảnh hư cấu | Gợi ý | Quy tắc |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | Nhi admitted ___ the wrong label. | `admit + verb-ing` | R3 |
| 11 | The archivist denied ___ the old map. | `deny + verb-ing` | R3 |
| 12 | The interns mentioned ___ the file name. | `mention + verb-ing` | R3 |
| 13 | The technician recommended ___ the cable first. | `recommend + verb-ing` | R3 |
| 14 | The organiser apologised for ___ the room number. | named `for` preposition + *-ing* | R4 |
| 15 | The neighbours complained about ___ the rehearsal. | named `complain about` + *-ing* | R4 |
| 16 | The treasurer insisted on ___ every receipt. | named `insist on` + *-ing* | R4 |
| 17 | The reviewer objected to ___ the final chart. | named `object to`; *to* is a preposition | R4 |
| 18 | The captain congratulated the crew on ___ the route. | `congratulate + object + on + verb-ing` | R5 |

<details><summary>Đáp án ẩn và lý do</summary>

10 **admitted copying**; 11 **denied moving**; 12 **mentioned changing**; 13 **recommended testing**; 14 **apologised for printing**; 15 **complained about hearing**; 16 **insisted on recording**; 17 **objected to removing**; 18 **congratulated the crew on completing**. R3: **admit** direct *-ing* (Oxford admit); **deny** direct *-ing* (Oxford deny); **mention** direct *-ing* (Oxford mention); **recommend** direct *-ing* (Oxford recommend); **suggest** direct *-ing* (Oxford suggest). R4: **apologise for** and **complain about** take *-ing* after the British Council dependent preposition; **insist on** (Oxford insist) and **object to** (Oxford object) use their named preposition. R5: **congratulate + object + on + -ing** is supported by Oxford congratulate and the British Council object-plus-preposition list. ID 18 keeps *the crew* as object; ID 17 treats *to* as a preposition.

</details>

### Tạo câu mới

Viết nhật ký hư cấu 80–100 từ về đội sửa bản đồ công viên; dùng ít nhất bốn mẫu R3/R4 và một R5. Tự chấm: -ing 5, giới từ 4, chủ thể 4, mạch lạc 4, tự sửa 3; đạt từ 14/20. Model tùy chọn:

<details><summary>Model key (90 từ hư cấu)</summary>

Yesterday our map team admitted losing one label and denied copying the old index. We mentioned checking every path before the review. The technician recommended testing the blue cable, while the organiser apologised for printing the wrong room number. Two neighbours complained about hearing the drill. The treasurer insisted on recording each receipt, and the captain congratulated the crew on completing the route before sunset. We then compared the new map with our notes and corrected three fictional place names before filing the final sketch.

</details>

## Buổi 3 — That-clause patterns (60 phút)

### Nhận diện và hoàn thành

Các tình huống hư cấu có mốc thời gian để lựa chọn có căn cứ. Làm bảng trước khi xem key.

| # | Bối cảnh hư cấu | Gợi ý | Quy tắc |
|---|---|---|---|
| 19 | At Monday’s design meeting, the committee suggested that the map ___ (show) both paths. | `suggest that + base`; *should* also possible | R6 |
| 20 | At yesterday’s shutdown briefing, the director demanded that the lights ___ (remain) off. | `demand that + base`; *should* also possible | R6 |
| 21 | Before last night’s opening, the safety lead insisted that the team ___ (check) the exits. | `insist that + base`; *should* also possible | R6 |
| 22 | In yesterday’s field note, the analyst claimed that the sample ___ (belong) to shelf B. | indicative tense follows the past report | R7 |
| 23 | At last month’s inspection, the curator admitted that the label ___ (contain) a typo. | indicative tense follows the past fact | R7 |
| 24 | During yesterday’s review, the volunteers agreed that the route ___ (need) a marker. | indicative tense follows the reported information | R7 |
| 25 | At yesterday’s 09:00 briefing, the mentor assured the trainees that the plan ___ (remain) private. | `assure + object + that`; future from past | R8 |
| 26 | At today’s 08:00 briefing, the guide reminded visitors that the bridge ___ (close) at dusk according to the timetable. | `remind + object + that`; timetable present | R8 |
| 27 | During yesterday’s inspection, the coordinator warned the crew that the floor ___ (be) slippery. | `warn + object + that`; past observation | R8 |

<details><summary>Đáp án ẩn, alternatives và lý do</summary>

19 **show**; **should show** is accepted. 20 **remain**; **should remain** is accepted. 21 **check**; **should check** is accepted. 22 **belonged** is preferred; **belongs** is accepted if still true. 23 **contained** is preferred; **contains** is accepted if the typo remains. 24 **needed** is preferred; **needs** is accepted if the route still needs a marker. 25 **would remain**; **remains** is accepted if privacy is still current. 26 **closes** follows the timetable; **would close** frames a future from the briefing. 27 **was**; **is** is accepted only if the floor is still slippery. R6/R8 keep their stated construction; R7 alternatives follow the reporting time and truth condition.

</details>

### Tạo câu mới

Viết 6 câu hư cấu về cuộc họp mở lại thư viện: hai R6, hai R7, hai R8; mỗi câu dùng reporting verb khác nhau. Tự chấm: pattern 6, thì/base 4, object 4, ngữ cảnh 3, tự sửa 3; đạt từ 14/20. Model tùy chọn:

<details><summary>Model key (6 câu hư cấu)</summary>

1. The chair suggested that the library open at noon.
2. The caretaker insisted that the team check every window.
3. The archivist claimed that the east room was ready.
4. The volunteers admitted that the sign contained an error.
5. The director assured the staff that the schedule would remain private.
6. The guide reminded visitors that the reading room closes at six.

</details>
