# Grammar 08 — Reported questions, requests and commands

> Ba buổi độc lập, mỗi buổi 60 phút. Bài luyện dùng người nói, người nghe và thời điểm rõ ràng trong bối cảnh hư cấu. Phạm vi chỉ là câu hỏi, lời yêu cầu và mệnh lệnh được tường thuật.

## Cách dùng trong ba buổi

Mỗi buổi: **10 phút nhận diện → 20 phút hoàn thành → 20 phút viết mới → 5 phút tự chấm → 5 phút lập lịch ôn**. Làm phần trống trước, ghi câu trả lời, rồi mới mở **Đáp án ẩn**. Tự chấm sau khi mở key; không dùng bài này để dự đoán band.

## Quy tắc nguồn ngắn gọn

- **Q1 — yes/no:** [EnglishClub Reported Questions](https://www.englishclub.com/grammar/sentence/reported-questions.php), introduction and YES/NO sections, explains *if/whether* and ordinary clause order. Keep the listener when supplied; do not use question inversion inside the reported clause.
- **Q2 — wh-question:** EnglishClub’s question-word section explains retained wh-words and ordinary clause order without question-form *do*. Oxford [ask](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/ask_1), question sense, separately supplies *ask somebody where/what*. Keep the wh-word, then subject before verb: *asked where I lived*, not *asked where did I live*.
- **Q3 — time and truth:** EnglishClub Backshift, body 23–34, 40–48, and 50–60, supports context-sensitive backshift: present simple → past simple, *can* → *could*, and *will* → *would*. A continuing or general truth may keep the present; *could, might, would,* and *should* do not backshift again.
- **Q4 — command:** Oxford [tell](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/tell) supports *tell somebody to do something*. Oxford [warn](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/warn) supports *warn somebody to do something* and *warn somebody not to do something* for danger or consequence. Keep the listener before *to* or *not to*.
- **Q5 — request:** Oxford [ask](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/ask_1) supports *ask somebody to do something*. *Please* marks politeness but does not change listener + *to + verb*; use *tell/order* only when the context supplies an instruction.

Đọc thêm: [British Council — reporting verbs with that, wh- and if clauses](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/reporting-verbs-wh-if-clauses), [EnglishClub — Backshift in Reported Speech](https://www.englishclub.com/grammar/sentence/reported-speech-backshift.php), [Oxford — ask](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/ask_1), [Oxford — tell](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/tell) và [Oxford — warn](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/warn). Ví dụ và model dưới đây là hư cấu, do bài viết mới.

## Buổi 1 — Yes/no questions (60 phút)

### Nhận diện và hoàn thành

Điền phần còn thiếu. Viết câu tường thuật đầy đủ; sửa lỗi đảo trợ động từ nếu có.

| # | Bối cảnh hư cấu: người nói → người nghe, thời điểm | Gợi ý | Quy tắc |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | At 09:00 today, Linh asked Nam, “Is the west door locked?” The door is still locked at 10:00. Linh asked Nam ___ the west door was locked. | if/whether + statement order | Q1/Q3 |
| 2 | Yesterday, the curator asked the guard, “Did the courier arrive?” The report concerns yesterday only. The curator asked ___ the courier had arrived. | if + backshift | Q1/Q3 |
| 3 | During Monday’s rehearsal, Mai asked the stage manager, “Will the lights work tonight?” The question was about that evening. Mai asked ___ the lights would work that night. | if + future from past | Q1/Q3 |
| 4 | At lunch, An asked Vy, “Are the labels dry now?” They remain dry. An asked Vy ___ the labels were dry. | object + if | Q1/Q3 |
| 5 | This morning, the librarian asked the volunteers, “Have you returned the keys?” The keys were returned before the question. She asked ___ they had returned the keys. | whether + statement order | Q1 |
| 6 | Last night, the guide asked me, “Can you read the map?” I still can. The guide asked me ___ I could read the map. | no auxiliary inversion | Q1/Q3 |
| 7 | At today’s briefing, the coach asked, “Does the route open at six?” The timetable still says six. The coach asked ___ the route opens at six. | present continuing timetable | Q1/Q3 |
| 8 | On Tuesday, Nhi wondered, “Will the archive be open tomorrow?” Tuesday was the reference point. Nhi wondered ___ the archive would be open the next day. | wonder + if | Q1 |
| 9 | At 14:00, the technician asked the team, “Did the alarm stop?” The question referred to the alarm at 13:50. The technician asked the team ___ the alarm had stopped. | statement order | Q1 |

<details><summary>Đáp án ẩn và lý do</summary>

1 **if/whether the west door was locked**; **if/whether the west door is locked** is accepted because it remains locked. 2 **if the courier had arrived**. 3 **if the lights would work that night**. 4 **if the labels were dry**; **if the labels are dry** is accepted because they remain dry. 5 **whether they had returned the keys**. 6 **if I could read the map**; **if I can read the map** is accepted because the ability still holds. 7 **if the route opens at six**. 8 **if the archive would be open the next day**. 9 **if the alarm had stopped**. Q1 keeps *if/whether*, listener, and statement order; Q3 chooses backshift from the reference time and permits present when the fact continues.

</details>

### Tạo câu mới

Viết 5 câu hư cấu: hai yes/no questions với *if*, hai với *whether*, và một câu giữ hiện tại vì lịch vẫn đúng. Tự chấm: reporting verb 5, if/whether 5, statement order 4, thời điểm 3, người nói/nghe 3; đạt từ 14/20.

<details><summary>Model key (5 câu hư cấu, tùy chọn)</summary>

1. At noon, Rina asked Joel if the museum was open.
2. Yesterday, the editor asked whether the parcel had arrived.
3. At the morning briefing, the guide wondered if the ferry would leave at six.
4. This afternoon, the curator asked whether the labels were ready.
5. The coordinator asked if the library opens at nine because the timetable is still current.

</details>

## Buổi 2 — Wh-questions and word order (60 phút)

### Nhận diện và hoàn thành

The wh-word stays; the reported clause uses statement order. Identify the speaker, listener, and reference time before completing each item.

| # | Bối cảnh hư cấu: người nói → người nghe, thời điểm | Gợi ý | Quy tắc |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | At 08:00 yesterday, Huy asked Lan, “Where is the spare key?” The location remained relevant at reporting time. Huy asked Lan where the spare key ___. | is/was by meaning | Q2/Q3 |
| 11 | Last Friday, the curator asked the guide, “When did the exhibit close?” The closing was last Friday. The curator asked when the exhibit ___. | had closed | Q2 |
| 12 | At the current briefing, Mai asked, “Why are the lights flashing?” They are still flashing. Mai asked why the lights ___. | are flashing | Q2/Q3 |
| 13 | Yesterday, the manager asked me, “What will the team change?” The change was future from yesterday. The manager asked what the team ___. | would change | Q2 |
| 14 | At 10:00 today, the visitor asked, “How far is the gallery?” The gallery has not moved. The visitor asked how far the gallery ___. | is/was | Q2/Q3 |
| 15 | During Monday’s call, researcher Mina asked assistant Lan, “Who did you invite?” The invitation list was completed then. Mina asked who Lan ___. | had invited | Q2 |
| 16 | This morning, Tâm asked, “Which room does the workshop use?” The schedule still names Room 4. Tâm asked which room the workshop ___. | uses | Q2/Q3 |
| 17 | Last night, the guide asked, “How long have the visitors waited?” The waiting ended before reporting. The guide asked how long the visitors ___. | had waited | Q2 |
| 18 | At today’s review, the editor asked, “What does this symbol mean?” The symbol still has the same meaning. The editor asked what the symbol ___. | means | Q2/Q3 |

<details><summary>Đáp án ẩn và lý do</summary>

10 **was/is**: *was* reports yesterday; *is* keeps the location current. 11 **had closed** and 17 **had waited** precede the earlier report. 12 **are still flashing**, 16 **uses**, and 18 **means** retain present because the context continues. 13 **would change** is future from yesterday; 14 **is/was** follows the same viewpoint choice. Keep the wh-word, then subject before verb: *where the key is*, not *where is the key* (Q2).

</details>

### Tạo câu mới

Viết nhật ký hư cấu 80–100 từ về một nhóm kiểm kê thư viện. Dùng ít nhất bốn wh-words, một câu giữ hiện tại vì sự thật còn đúng, một câu backshift, và nêu rõ ai hỏi ai khi thời điểm không phải hiện tại. Tự chấm: wh-word 4, statement order 5, thì/ý nghĩa 5, speaker-listener-time 3, tự sửa 3; đạt từ 14/20.

<details><summary>Model key (90 từ hư cấu)</summary>

Yesterday our library team recorded what the archivist asked and what the volunteers answered. She asked where the missing atlas was, and Minh checked the north cabinet. The coordinator asked why the scanner was silent, but it is working now. We noted when the courier had arrived and how long the delivery had taken. At today’s review, Lan asked which shelf the atlas belongs to because the catalogue still uses that location. Everyone wrote the questions in statement order and marked the speaker and listener before filing the fictional report.

</details>

## Buổi 3 — Requests and commands (60 phút)

### Nhận diện và hoàn thành

Distinguish a request from an order by the reporting verb and context. Keep the listener as object; use *to + verb* or *not to + verb*.

| # | Bối cảnh hư cấu: người nói → người nghe, thời điểm | Gợi ý | Quy tắc |
|---|---|---|---|
| 19 | At 09:00, the curator said to the assistant, “Please label the blue folder.” The assistant was the listener. The curator asked the assistant ___. | request + to | Q5 |
| 20 | During yesterday’s safety drill, the supervisor said to the visitors, “Do not touch the wet display.” The supervisor told the visitors ___. | negative command | Q4 |
| 21 | At Monday’s meeting, the director said to Minh, “Move the chairs before noon.” The director ordered Minh ___. | order + object + to | Q4 |
| 22 | This morning, Nhi said to the technician, “Could you check the projector, please?” Nhi asked the technician ___. | polite request | Q5 |
| 23 | Last night, the guide said to the group, “Please wait by the gate.” The guide asked the group ___. | request, not command | Q5 |
| 24 | At 16:00, the coach said to the players, “Do not leave the court.” The coach told the players ___. | not to | Q4 |
| 25 | Yesterday, the librarian said to Hoa, “Please return the atlas tomorrow.” The librarian asked Hoa ___. | time from yesterday | Q5 |
| 26 | At today’s rehearsal, the stage manager said to the crew, “Keep the aisle clear.” The stage manager told the crew ___. | positive instruction | Q4 |
| 27 | During the current inspection, Officer Lin is narrating the event in first person. I asked the guard, “Please show me the visitor list.” I asked the guard ___. | request keeps recipient | Q5 |

<details><summary>Đáp án ẩn và lý do</summary>

19 **to label the blue folder**, 22 **to check the projector**, 23 **to wait by the gate**, 25 **to return the atlas the next day**, and 27 **to show me the visitor list** use Q5: listener + *to*. *Please* marks politeness. 20 **not to touch the wet display** and 24 **not to leave the court** use Q4 negative instruction. 21 **to move the chairs before noon** and 26 **to keep the aisle clear** use the positive instruction pattern. Q4’s *tell* and *warn* meanings depend on instruction or danger; item 27 remains a request because no warning is given.

</details>

### Tạo câu mới

Viết 6 câu hư cấu về cuộc mở cửa lại thư viện: hai reported requests, hai positive commands, và hai negative commands. Dùng reporting verb khác nhau, nêu rõ listener, và giữ các mốc thời gian cần thiết. Tự chấm: request/command 6, object 4, to/not to 4, context 3, tự sửa 3; đạt từ 14/20.

<details><summary>Model key (6 câu hư cấu, tùy chọn)</summary>

1. At 08:00, the archivist asked Mai to unlock the reading room.
2. Yesterday, the coordinator requested the volunteers to check the lamps.
3. The director told the team to move the desks before visitors arrived.
4. The guide ordered the guard to keep the side gate closed.
5. The librarian told the children not to enter the storage room.
6. At today’s inspection, the curator warned the crew not to touch the wet paint.

</details>
