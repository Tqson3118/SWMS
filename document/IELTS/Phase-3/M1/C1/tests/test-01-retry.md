## Grammar (50)
Câu1–6 điền;7–9 sửa nếu cần (câu đã đúng được giữ);10 nối câu. Mỗi câu5 điểm.
1. The member ___ arrived early brought a pencil.
2. The message ___ she sent was clear.
3. Amir, ___ lives nearby, opens the room.
4. The colleague ___ plan we followed is here.
5. The café ___ they talked was noisy.
6. The day ___ the club opened was sunny.
7. Leo, that is a volunteer, helps us.
8. The book that Maya read was useful.
9. The office which she works is small.
10. Join: I met a student. Her notes were clear.
## Vocabulary (30)
Viết đúng từ tiếng Anh, mỗi câu3 điểm:
1 đáng tin cậy;2 giả định;3 linh hoạt;4 nét đặc trưng;5 biết cân nhắc người khác;6 quyết đoán;7 bối cảnh;8 khuynh hướng;9 đồng cảm;10 nhất quán.
## Listening (10)
