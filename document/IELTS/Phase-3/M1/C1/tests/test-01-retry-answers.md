# Key — test-01-retry
## Grammar
1. **who/that** — subject person.
2. **which/that/omitted** — object defining.
3. **who** — non-defining.
4. **whose** — possession.
5. **where/in which** — place.
6. **when/on which** — time.
7. **Leo, who is a volunteer, helps us.** — non-defining who.
8. **The book that/which Maya read was useful; The book Maya read was useful.** — original valid; object optional.
9. **The office where/in which she works is small; The office which she works in is small.** — work in place.
10. **I met a student whose notes were clear.** — whose + noun.
5 điểm/câu đúng hoàn toàn; câu sửa7–10:3 điểm đúng role/đại từ và2 điểm cấu trúc/dấu phẩy còn lại. Chấp nhận các diễn đạt đúng cùng nghĩa. Câu2/8 cho phép bỏ object pronoun; không bỏ subject pronoun.
