# Key — test-01
## Grammar
1. **who/that** — subject, person.
2. **which/that/omitted** — object, thing, defining.
3. **who** — non-defining, person.
4. **whose** — possession + noun.
5. **where/in which** — place, meet there.
6. **when/in which** — time.
7. **My only notebook, which I bought yesterday, is blue.** — that cannot introduce this non-defining clause.
8. **The person who/whom/that she helped was grateful; The person she helped was grateful.** — valid original; object can be omitted.
9. **The room where/in which we meet is upstairs; The room which we meet in is upstairs.** — meet needs place/preposition.
10. **Amir owns a notebook whose cover is green.** — whose + cover.
5 điểm/câu đúng hoàn toàn; câu sửa7–10:3 điểm đúng role/đại từ và2 điểm cấu trúc/dấu phẩy còn lại. Chấp nhận các diễn đạt đúng cùng nghĩa. Câu2/8 cho phép bỏ object pronoun; không bỏ subject pronoun.
