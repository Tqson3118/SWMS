# 📖 Đọc 4 — Who Am I? (80 từ)

---

Who am I? I am a person. I have a body, a face, and a mind. My mind thinks. My heart feels. My hands do things.

I am kind, but sometimes I am jealous. I am honest, but sometimes I tell small lies. I am strong with my friends, but shy with strangers. I am happy most days, but lonely some nights.

People are not simple. A person can be brave and scared at the same time. A person can love someone and feel angry with them on the same day. This is what makes us human.

---

## Câu hỏi
1. What does the mind do? What does the heart do?
2. Can a person feel two opposite things at once? Give an example.
3. "This is what makes us ______" — điền từ còn thiếu.

**ĐÁP ÁN:**
1. The mind thinks; the heart feels.
2. Yes. Example: brave and scared at the same time (or: love someone and feel angry with them on the same day).
3. human.
