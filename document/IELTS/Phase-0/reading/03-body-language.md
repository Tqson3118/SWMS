# 📖 Đọc 3 — Body Language (70 từ)

---

People talk with words, but also with their body. A smile means happy. A tear means sad. When a person is nervous, their hands move fast. When a person is calm, their body is quiet.

Look at the face. The eyes show the truth. A friend says "I am fine", but her eyes look sad. Maybe she is not fine. The mouth can lie, but the eyes cannot.

A hug is a kind of body language too. When words are hard, a hug says: "I am here for you."

---

## Câu hỏi
1. Give two examples of body language in the text.
2. What shows the truth, according to the text?
3. What does a hug say?

**ĐÁP ÁN:**
1. A smile, a tear (or: nervous hands, calm body).
2. The eyes.
3. "I am here for you."

> Lưu ý từ mới: **mean, nervous, quiet, lie**. Câu "The mouth can lie, but the eyes cannot" — câu tâm lý đầu tiên bạn thuộc lòng.
