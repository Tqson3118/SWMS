# 📖 Đọc 1 — How Are You? (50 từ)

> Đọc 2 lần. Lần 1: đọc trôi chảy, không dịch. Lần 2: đọc to, đánh dấu từ chưa hiểu.

---

Hi! My name is Mai. I am from Vietnam. I am a student. I am happy today because I learn English.

My English teacher is very kind. She has a big smile. Her name is Anna. Anna is from England.

How are you today? Are you happy? Are you sad? I hope you are happy.

---

## Câu hỏi (viết đáp án ra giấy)

1. Where is Mai from?
2. How does she feel today? Why?
3. What is the teacher's name?

---

**ĐÁP ÁN:**
1. She is from Vietnam.
2. She is happy because she learns English.
3. Anna.

> Từ mới đáng chú ý: **student, kind, smile, hope** — thêm vào Anki nếu chưa biết.
