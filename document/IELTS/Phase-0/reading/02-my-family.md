# 📖 Đọc 2 — My Family (60 từ)

---

My family is small. I have a mother, a father, and one sister. My mother is kind and calm. My father is strong and smart. My sister is young and shy.

We live in a small home. Every evening, we eat together. My mother cooks. My father tells a story. My sister laughs. I feel happy and safe at home.

A family is a warm place. In a good family, every person listens and every person cares.

---

## Câu hỏi
1. How many people are in Mai's family?
2. What does the father do every evening?
3. What is a "good family" for the writer?

**ĐÁP ÁN:**
1. Four people (mother, father, sister, Mai).
2. He tells a story.
3. A place where every person listens and cares.

> Chủ đề này là nền cho bài "attachment" (sự gắn bó) sau này — ghi nhớ cảm giác "warm, safe".
