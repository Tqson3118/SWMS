# 📖 Đọc 5 — Feelings (90 từ)

---

Feelings come and go. In the morning, I feel happy. In the evening, I feel tired. Today I am excited; tomorrow I may be nervous. Feelings are like the weather: they change, and that is fine.

When I feel sad, I cry. Crying is not bad. A tear carries the feeling outside, and then the heart is lighter. When I feel angry, I breathe slow. A slow breath makes the body calm.

Some people hide their feelings. They say "I am fine" but they are not fine. Hidden feelings become heavy. A good friend listens, so feelings do not have to hide. That is why friendship is good for the heart.

---

## Câu hỏi
1. What are feelings like? Why is that fine?
2. According to the text, why is crying not bad?
3. Why is friendship good for the heart?

**ĐÁP ÁN:**
1. Like the weather — they change, and that is fine.
2. Because a tear carries the feeling outside, and then the heart is lighter.
3. Because a good friend listens, so feelings do not have to hide (don't become heavy).

> 3 câu tâm lý đẹp trong bài — đọc to 5 lần câu cuối của mỗi đoạn. Đây là "first taste" chủ đề tâm lý của lộ trình.
